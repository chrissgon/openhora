Interface

Your projects, as a small city

A person opens a local page and sees their projects as a small low-poly city: one building per project, one floor per area agent, an owl at a desk on each floor, and a card for whatever is waiting for the person. It is the view onto the task runtime, not a separate product.

The real interface is a local page that the openhora runtime serves on your own machine, at 127.0.0.1 only. The tour below is a demo of it on fictional data.

The owl's three states

  • Waiting

    faces the person; one wing raised and waving. No question mark.

  • Working

    turned three-quarters to its desk; both wings on the keyboard, tapping in turn.

  • Idle

    standing, eyes shut, breathing slowly, three small z rising.

“The owl is not a progress indicator.”

04

Who holds the power

the page can only take power away

From the page, a person can stop an agent or lower its mode at once; widening what an agent may do is done only in the terminal.

fictional Engineering agent · northwind-shop

Pick a mode

A move down applies at once. A move up is refused here: a wider mode is set in the terminal.

A wider mode is set in the terminal, not on this page:

terminalon your machine
$ python3 runtime/cli.py set-mode --project <dir> --agent engineering --mode autonomous# code never accepts a move up: accept the new hash, after reading the file$ python3 runtime/cli.py accept-config --project <dir> --sha256 <hash>

05

Light, dark, phone

One colour-mode button cycles System, Light, Dark; the icon (monitor, sun, moon) shows which. System, the default, follows the system's scheme. Nothing is stored: a reload starts on System.

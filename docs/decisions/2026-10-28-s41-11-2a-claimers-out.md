## 2026-10-28: the tier-1 claimers are out and the interpreter reads the line (S41-11 PR 2a; no model call, no provider call, no lab run, fingerprint unchanged)

The maintainer decided, on 2026-10-28, and this change makes it true: the closed forms that took a line of words before any model (the write families' op-less read, the reminders' natural claim, `natural.match` for reads, notices, acts and brakes) are no longer a claim of the bridge, of the operations layer or of the interpreter's pipeline. The decisions said in words (approve, reject, release, answer, go-ahead) stay closed code (`natural.decision`); `/remind <free words>` stays (`reminder-form`, narrowed); the help form moved to `greeting.py`.

**Decided with this change (translated from the chat of 2026-10-28).**
- **OPEN-A11-2 (brakes).** A brake in words goes through the first leg, which takes no model slot (`decide-without-slot`); during an outage it is the typed hint. A brake said by a sender the interpreter does not read is decided in that leg too (review C1-a), and the first leg asks the policy for `say` with the kind `interpret` so that `interpret` alone reaches the interpreter and the refusal after it is the operations layer's (C1-b, decision #6).
- **OPEN-A11-3.** A page, terminal, MCP or chat line in words reaches the planning agent (the interpreter reads messaging senders only): 39 rows of the golden (`write-form-ops` 25, `tier-1-form` 14).
- **OPEN-A11-5.** The stand-in answers from M41-5's run-1 answers keyed by item id (`evals/interpreter/replay/or-deepseek-v4.1-flash/m41-5-2026-10-28/`), else the gold of the line; the fallbacks and the pinned replay clock are in `runtime/tests/entry_kit.py` (`COVER_GOLD_ONLY`, `REPLAY_CLOCK`).
- **C1-c ("Responder com o comando digitado", the recommended answer).** A sender the interpreter does not read who says a reminder or a calendar line gets the typed-command sentence (`IX_TYPE_REMIND`: "type /remind <time> <text>"; `IX_TYPE_WRITE`), and the line goes to no model and not to the planning agent: the row `typed-private-line`.
- **Time doubt ("Pergunta específica A ou B", the recommended answer).** Where the code's time reader doubts a reminder's time, the engine's own question is asked ("Which time do you mean: A or B?", or the reading to confirm); the generic "say them again, with the day and the hour" only when the engine has nothing to offer.

**What the review of the rows asked and this change does.** The one-tap Yes button and the `reminder` ledger card come back for the reminder questions of the interpreter's carry-out (C2-d); "no reminder N waits for you" is a plain sentence (C2-e); the engine reads the moment first (C2-f, BL7-4's deadline rule); a text made only of the trigger words ("me lembra") is no text (C2-g); the cover answers are judged at the pinned replay clock of the world, never the wall clock (B1); items that disagree on a line take the one clocked as the replay (B2); the guard, composite and reply rows take the gold for a line their pre-state also says (B3); the outage brake records no model call (the breaker is open in its pre-state).

**Rows and tests.** `write-form`, `write-form-ops` and `say-natural` are retired (`turn_order.RETIRED`, L63's "Replaced so far"); `say-natural` had the effect of `say` (a typed `/remind` is taken by `reminder-form`). The golden is re-recorded under the guard phrase for the 583 base rows and the seven extension packages that moved, with `answers_sha256` named in the `changes` entry; the list, per retired row, with five new outcomes each and every visible change, is in the pull request. The builder of the tier-1 cover set (`scripts/interpreter_corpus_tier1.py`) is deleted with the claimers it ran, and the two files are pinned data. The tests of deleted claimers go with their modules (T9), each listed in the pull request with its reason; no test named in the contract rows L51, L54 or L60 to L65 is cut (the ones that entered through a claim are retargeted through `closed_heads.py`, the interpreter's stand-in).

**Not done here (2b).** `natural.match`, `natural.candidate`, `natural.private`'s old oracle (kept for the tests as `closed_heads.old_private`), `requests.forms` and `scripts/registry_build.py` stay until their last reader is gone; the generated mirror of the contract is regenerated by the keeper after this merges. "para a tarefa N" read as "show task N" by a model is a model finding, not a claimer: it is a separate package.

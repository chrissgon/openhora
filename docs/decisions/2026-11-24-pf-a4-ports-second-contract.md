## 2026-11-24: The five kept ports on the ffmpeg engine under the second contract (PF-a4; plan video quality v6)

**Status: draft of PF-a4a (the host code). PF-a4b adds the five recorded determinism tests, the measured numbers and M2.**

**Context.** After C10 five ports stay, all ours: `hf:caption-highlight`, `hf:caption-pill-karaoke` (caption), `hf:count-up` (number), `hf:spring-pop` (keyword) and `hf:testimonial-card` (quote). Today only the HyperFrames engine draws them. PF-a4 makes the ffmpeg engine draw them under the second page contract, so that retiring the old engine (PF-b) loses neither the look nor the image.

**Decision.**
- **M1, the maintainer's words:** "Sim, com esse portão (Recomendado)", option (a): the two caption ports run on the second contract. (validate: allow english-only -- the maintainer's own words, quoted)
- **Caption instruction, the maintainer's words (2026-11-24):** "Um ajuste. Sobre a questao da legenda nos videos. Vamos tirar a regra dela nao aparecer sobre o rosto, já vi videos que isso acontece e nao sao ruins. É comum em estilos que a pessoa se movimenta muito e o rosto muda muito de posicao" (validate: allow english-only -- the maintainer's own words, quoted)
- **Face instruction, the maintainer's words (2026-11-24):** "Pode tirar a regra do rosto para tudo. Pode haver animacoes que exibam conteudos acima do rosto do usuario, e tudo bem" (validate: allow english-only -- the maintainer's own words, quoted)
- **What the two instructions change.** The principle is "limit security, not creativity": a face rule is not a security limit. No overlay is refused any more for touching the face box (a card, a caption, a pip card, an admitted registry block). The face box stays a **placement** input: the bands, where a card goes, the default zones. Removed: the alpha face gate of the second contract (`_alpha_gate`: R3-2 in the PF-a2 trade listed it as a kept guard, and M1's trade was made on it; it is no longer one), the box test of the renderAt frame loop (`_layout.face_hit`, which is deleted), the old engine's box rule (`_hf_gate.check_boxes`: no `overlay_hits_face`, no `frame`-zone span, an unknown zone is `box_zone_unknown`), and the schema rule that an entry of the second contract must have `may_cross_face` false (the key stays: it is pinned in every block file and read by the catalogue's readers; it is now a boolean that only the old engine's pixel check reads, to leave out the frames of a full-frame layout). The code `overlay_hits_face` no longer exists.
- **The guards that remain** on the second contract: the container (it sees pixels only, no network); the page policy with `connect-src 'none'` and the request interception; the hash pin of the fragment; the recorded determinism test (three forward runs and one reverse run, expiring on any change of the port, the page shape, GSAP, the runtime, a shim, the host or the browser); the escape lint and, for our own ports, the fragment lint (`_gsap_lint.check`); the band clip (`contain: paint; overflow: clip`); the RGBA frame check; and the line gate (no alpha at or below the line: the caption band's top for a card, its bottom, 1560 on the presets, for a caption chunk; the cards keep 1560 and the band clip). A caption chunk of six frames or more must draw something (`block_not_drawn`; the floor is the old engine's).
- **What is still refused on the old engine, and why it is not removed here.** `_hf_gate.pixel_check` compares the face region with the undrawn base by PSNR (`face_pixels_changed`). It also guards the parity of the base picture with the ffmpeg engine, it is the one check that sees a block drawing with no box, and the engine and its gate are deleted in PF-b, so it stays and is reported. An overlay on the face is therefore drawn by the ffmpeg engine and refused by the old one until PF-b.
- **The choice (B).** The `gsap` runtime on the ffmpeg engine, under the second contract's page mechanics: one `/h/` page per caption chunk and per beat (`_hf_port.py`), built with the head of an admitted registry block's page; the fragment is mounted through a local `<template>` (the shape the PF-a2 and PF-a3 live legs proved), seeked by the runtime. Options (A) a tier-A allowlist entry per port (bends the provenance rule and cannot carry a list of words) and (C) rewriting the five as `renderat` pages (re-doing the GSAP eases by hand, five look reviews) were not chosen.
- **The guards, port against registry block.** Hash pin: `pins.json` pins `gsap.html` and `block.json`, checked at load and again when the page is built. Provenance: our repository; `admit-block --port` refuses a working file that differs from its pin (`block_hash`) or from `HEAD` (`admitted_commit`). Served digest: `served_digest({gsap.html, port-host})`, so a change of `PORT_HOST_VERSION` expires every port record. Lints: `_hf_lint.check` and `_gsap_lint.check`. The record lives in the port's `block.json` (optional at load, so the old engine still loads a port with none; required for the ffmpeg engine to list or draw it: `usable`, `_blocks.py`). Runtime hash: the same value as an admitted block's.
- **What PF-a4a changes.** Catalogue rules (`SECOND_CONTRACT`, `usable`, an optional record on a tier-B `gsap` block, the caption resolved like the beats); `_hf_port.py`; `_hf_host.prepare` builds caption and beat pages; `_cdp.Page.close`; `_frames` draws the caption track as `cap_*` and closes a track's previous page; `_render.admitted_ranges` and the gates per prefix; `ffmpeg.py admit-block --port` and `_inner.py admit-check --port`, `_admit_check.CAPTION_LINES`; `engines/ffmpeg.json` runs `renderat`, `hf`, `gsap`; samples for the three card ports; the notice of the five ports moves to `hf-runtime/NOTICE.md`. No port has a record, so no production render changes.
- **Order fix.** `render` now settles the pips and splits before it builds the second contract's pages (`compose_plan` settles them again, with the same result): a split cuts a caption chunk into pieces and a pip gives it a band, and the pages must be built for those, not for the chunks before.
- **Host-level font family.** The page's font check walks every element that holds text, the fragment's own `<style>` and `<script>` included; they inherit from the composition root, so the host sets `font-family: var(--font-text)` there (otherwise the browser's own default serif is `font_not_allowed`). No port file is touched.

**What it saves and what it loses (principle 13).** It saves retiring the old engine without losing the look he chose, and without a second image or browser, and it removes a rule that was not security (and a gate that refused a look he finds common and good). It loses: a port page parses the 627 KB runtime once per caption chunk (cost in "Not verified"); every port record expires on any runtime, shim, browser or port change; the caption look over a pip or a split is new; and the alpha face gate listed as a kept guard by R3-2 is gone (the remaining guards are above).

**Verified.** Offline, with the host's Chromium and ffmpeg and the stand-in runtime: a fictional card port and caption port drawn twice byte-identical with their proofs in the summary; a port with no record refused on the ffmpeg engine and a record at another runtime refused as not proven; a changed fragment is `block_hash`; the page of a port against a golden, its escaping, bands and duration; the frame loop's caption track and page closing with fake pages; the gates per prefix; the line gate and no face gate on synthetic RGBA frames.

**Not verified** (the supervisor's live steps, PF-a4b): that the real runtime mounts the port template and seeks it (S4); that each real port passes the reverse run (S5, the raster-history risk); the cost of a page per caption chunk; the look of the two caption ports over a pip band or a split piece; the old engine's refusal of an overlay on the face (`face_pixels_changed`) in the live leg F5; two of the five ports' `gsap.html` comments still name the old notice file (see the PR).

## 2026-11-10: Punches on emphasis and the colour effects flash, dip, mono and tint, with the grade inside the face check's reference (V9a-3; the V9a spec, section 6; STOP C answered)

**Decision.** The script's `emphasis: punch` is drawn, and the review gains five effects over word ranges: `punch`, `flash`, `dip`, `mono` and `tint`. They touch the base picture only, never a zone, and no gate number moves (`PIXEL_MIN_PSNR_DB`, the zones, the 1560 line and `card_min_height` are as they were; a test reads them).

- **Rows, not filters.** `_edl.resolve_timeline` turns the words into whole-frame rows (`punches`, `grades`, `effects_dropped`). A punch spans its words, stretched to 0.8 s and cut at 3 s and at the end of its kept range; its zoom is the range's own level at the punch's first frame times 1.12, held for the span and capped at the zoom's `factor_max` (1.25), a hard jump in and out. A flash lasts 4 frames and a dip 6 from the first frame of its first word; mono and tint span their words, clipped to 0.5 to 4 s and never across a cut. `_edl.range_effects` makes one table by kept range, in the range's own frame index, and the ffmpeg engine's graph (`_media.base_graph`), the HyperFrames page (`_hf_page.build_parts`) and the face check's reference (`_hf_gate.reference_graph`) all read that table.
- **Defaults (OPEN-3).** Script punches are drawn by default; every review effect is off until the person adds it (`captions-edit`, op `effect`). A script punch the person drops at review (`{"op": "effect", "id": "p<beat>", "remove": true}`) is kept in the review's `dropped_punches` and removed from beats.json at every alignment.
- **Parameters (OPEN-7).** Punch 1.12 of the range's level, capped at 1.25, 0.8 to 3 s.

**The maintainer's answer (OPEN-8 / STOP C), 2026-11-10, verbatim:**

> "Comparar com o efeito aplicado (Recomendado)" (validate: allow english-only -- the maintainer's own words, quoted)

The question was whether the face check, which refuses a render when the face pixels change too much, should compare against a reference that already has the same colour effect applied (so it still catches any drawing on the face), with the effect bounded in duration. He chose to compare against the reference with the effect applied.

**What that costs, and the bounds in code (principle 13).** The check no longer proves the face is unchanged on the frames of a grade. It proves that both engines drew the same picture there and that nothing else was drawn on the face. The bounds: at most 6 review effects; a flash of at most 4 frames and a dip of at most 6, at most two of them (flashes and dips together) in a video (`too_many_flashes`); mono and tint at most 4 s each; two grades never share a word (`effect_overlap`); a flash, dip or tint that would start inside the one before it is dropped (`grade_overlap`). The numbers are constants of `_edl.py`; `renderer-presets.json` may lower them and never raise them (frame counts, seconds and the alphas are clamped in code: flash and dip at most 0.85 a frame, tint at most 0.18). What it saves: a colour effect over the face, which the references show in 16% of the shorts, would otherwise fail every render by design.

**Mono by the CSS matrix.** The ffmpeg engine's `colorchannelmixer` copies the matrix of `grayscale(1)` (`.2126 .7152 .0722` in every row), the page uses `filter: grayscale(1)`. `colorchannelmixer` reads RGB, so the range is converted with explicit bt709 matrices around it, in a range that has a mono row only; the other frames of that range go through the two conversions too and may differ from a pure copy by a level or two. Whether 30 dB holds on a grade's frames is the live leg L3; if it does not, the first remedy is the drawing method (for example a chroma-neutral filter, or an SVG `feColorMatrix` on the page), never a lower threshold.

**Departures from the spec, each small.**
- `providers/renderer/edl.schema.json` is edited (two properties, `punches` and `effects`, in `beats`): a test holds the schema's keys equal to `_edl.BEATS_KEYS`, so adding the two keys to the code required it. The file is in no other part's row.
- A punch whose cap would not zoom in past the range's own level (a zoom factor of 1.25 with a push-in) is dropped (`punch_capped`) instead of drawn as a zoom-out.
- `too_many_flashes` is also refused at the review (`runtime/captions.py`), not only at the renderer's check, so the person hears it at the edit.
- The drop of a script punch checks the beat number against 1 to 12 only: `ops_media_edit.py`, which calls `apply_edits`, is outside the V9a-3 row, so the set of the script's punch beats is not passed (`apply_edits(punches=...)` takes it when a later package passes it). The align step ignores a drop that names no punch.
- The page's grades are one `#fx` layer at the root, above the video wrappers and below every overlay, instead of a div inside each wrapper: one layer serves every range and sits where the spec asked for it in the stacking order. Mono is the wrapper's filter, as the spec says.
- A punched range's page zoom follows the frame-index rule of the filters (`z0 + (z1 - z0) * n / (frames - 1)`) instead of GSAP's linear tween over the duration, so the page and the reference agree to the frame; a range with no punch keeps its GSAP tween.

**Verified offline.** Resolution, the three drawings from one table, the golden bytes of the graph, the zoom filters and the page with no effect, the node-run drive code of the page, the review operations and their limits, and a host ffmpeg run of the graph on a synthetic pattern (mono frames carry no chroma). **Not verified:** a render in either engine, the PSNR of a grade's frames (L3), the cost in render time of a drive tween per punched range, how the effects look (the C10 sheets), and the real recording.

**Corrections after the first live leg and the review (same package).**
- `perspective` counts its `on` from 1 (frame i reads `on = i + 1`) while `zoompan` counts from 0, so the face check's reference, which zooms with `perspective`, switched a punch one frame before the page and the encoder: the pixel check refused at the span's first and last frame (psnr 20.9, range 0 frames 51 and 66). The reference's punch expression now reads `on-1` (`_media.zoom_filter_linear`). The diagnostics of a refusal now give the punch's zoom on the worst frame.
- Mono is applied before the colour layers in the ffmpeg graph, as the page draws it (a filter on the video under the `#fx` layer), so a tint or a flash over a grey frame stays coloured on both engines.
- A merge that would bring two grades into one word is refused at the review (`effect_overlap`, nothing changed).
- The renderer's `effects_dropped` (`punch_short`, `punch_capped`, `grade_short`, `grade_overlap`) is in the render summary of the ffmpeg engine, in the HyperFrames page result and render result (closed schema rows in `_docker.py`, `hyperframes.py`), and in the media step's render result as closed rows. These two host files were edited at the supervisor's request.

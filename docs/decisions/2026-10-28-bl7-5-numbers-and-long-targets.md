## 2026-10-28: a message number the model gives opens the family, and a long sender-and-subject target is grounded (BL7-5, limits L54, L61 and L63, no model call, no lab run)

The first blind sets showed two mail lines that the product refused although the model had understood them (SPEC-BL7-4 section 9, gaps G1 and G3). Package BL7-5 builds both; the prompt, the catalog, the protocol and the enabling entry are unchanged, and no rule of L54, L61 or L63 changes (L61 gains two sentences in its description).

- **G1, numbers.** `ops_interpret._carry_out` tries the closed forms first, as before. When they read nothing, the operation is a mail operation that names messages (`MAIL_TARGET_OPS`), the project's language is pt-BR or en and the model gave `message` or `messages`, `ops_natural_mail.mail_number_seed` opens the family with a seed `{"op", "by": "numbers", "slots": {"numbers": [...]}}` (a reply or a reply draft adds its body, a move its folder, a mark its state when the span reads one). A seed exists only when the decision's spans hold none of `target`, `day`, `to` or `subject`, and the numbers the model gave are EXACTLY the line's message numbers (`natural_writes_mail.free_numbers`). **The stricter rule of the review (supervisor, 2026-10-28, principle 13):** a number counts as a message number only when it is written `#n`, or follows a mail noun of the tables (`email 4`, `e-mails 2`, `mensagem 3`), or is chained to such a number by a joiner (`and`, `e`, `or`, `ou`, a comma) with an optional article between (`emails 2, the 5 or 7`); it never counts when it overlaps a text of the decision or a time phrase of `natural_time.find`, is followed by a unit, `o'clock` or a currency mark, or is preceded by a currency. A count noun ("the 2 budgets"), a year, a phone number, a room ("room 4", "sala 4") and a clock ("the 2 o'clock meeting") are no message number by construction. A range gives none: `1-3`, `1 a 3`, `1 to 3`, `do 1 ao 3`, `between 1 and 3`; and an unmappable time hit gives none. The numbers become `<source>#<n>` of the newest snapshot (origin `line-number`), a numbered line never refreshes a snapshot, a number past the end, a snapshot older than 26 hours and a caller without `ask-mail` are refused by `item_refs` when the operation runs, and a reply still goes only to the index's From (L60). `ops_natural_writes._read` accepts `by` `numbers` beside `target`. S41-11's inventory must list `mail_number_seed` as what replaces the numbered mail heads when it deletes the closed ones (L63 (3)). What the stricter rule costs: a line with bare numbers and no mail noun ("marca o 1 e o 2 como lido", "move 4 and 9 to the Receipts folder") is `IX_CANNOT_WRITE` again; the person names the e-mail ("marca o email 1 e o 2") or writes `#1`.
- **G3, the ground.** `interpreter._own_subject_split` grounds a mail `target` that the closed rule reads as no words (more than six content words) and that holds a subject marker after the sender (`SUBJECT_MARKERS`, from the second word on, with a word after it that is not itself the start of a marker: "Mei Su about ..." and "Lia Sur regarding ..." are names that are markers of another language, so the split is at "about" and "regarding"): the ground is the sender and the subject without the marker word (`_subject_ground`), at most `natural_writes_mail.GROUND_WORDS_MAX` (10) content words. It is recorded as `target_ground`, and the sender's part as `target_sender` (nothing reads it yet: OPEN-BL7-5-E, so that GROUNDING-FROM or a later package can require the sender's words in the From). `mail_target_seed` reads a ground with `ground_words`; `match_words` is untouched and still needs EVERY word, so the cap only decides between a search and a refusal and the ground never reaches a message with fewer of the target's words. `target_words` gained a keyword `cap` and its output with no `cap` is byte-identical (20 recorded targets in `test_mail_long_target.py`).
- **The mirror.** `interpreter_pipeline.mail_step` follows both steps in the product's order (`_numbers_step`, `ground_words`); `test_interpreter_card_parity.py` proves it (G1 and G3 rows are cards now, and eight new rows pin the numbers path, including a project language with no write forms).

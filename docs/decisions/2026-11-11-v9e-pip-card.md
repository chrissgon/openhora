## 2026-11-11: the pip is an image card over the speaker (V9e; no measurement change, no skill changed, no lab run)

Package V9e of the video plan. The vocabulary (`shared/references/video-presets.md`, `### pip`) says the pip shows a picture or a clip the person uploads, in a
bubble over the speaker, who stays on screen. The renderer drew the inverse: the asset full frame and the speaker in a round bubble. The maintainer watched a
demo render on 2026-11-11 and said, translated: "the pip came out poor". He chose the recommendation, to make the pip follow the reference videos ("Siga com o recomendado").

### What the reference videos show (read by the supervisor: 69 short videos and 44 vertical clips, with labels and one-frame-per-second grids)

- The image in a card over the speaker, who stays full frame: 15 events in 4 videos. The card is a rectangle with rounded corners, between half and three quarters of the frame's width, below the face (chest height), centred or in a lower corner; the face is never covered.
- The speaker small over the image: 2 videos.
- A split screen: 17 events in 9 videos. Out of scope; recorded in `docs/backlog.md` (V9E-SPLIT-SCREEN).
- No reference shows a round bubble.

### Decisions

- **D1. The speaker stays full frame.** The full-frame asset, the round bubble and the circle mask are gone; the picture under a pip is the cut and zoomed recording, punches and grades included.
- **D2. The card.** The asset goes whole into a rounded rectangle (contain), over the speaker: at most the safe width (950 px) wide and `pip.card_max_height` high, drawn in ffmpeg as the bubble was (scale to the box, a rounded-rectangle `geq` mask the size of the box, overlay with `enable` on the beat's frames); one path for an image and a clip. The page draws the soft shadow outside the card's edges and the label chip on its top-left corner; the box stays transparent.
- **D3. Numbers, in `renderer-presets.json`.** `pip.card_max_height` 640 (a square card is 59 percent of the width, a 16:9 one 950 by 534), `pip.card_min_height` 240, `presets.pip.looks.radius_px` 24, `presets.pip.looks.shadow` `0 14px 40px rgba(0,0,0,0.55)`. The minimum was 300 in the spec and is 240 by the supervisor's amendment: under the default face box (y 620, h 560) 224 px are free below the face, so the pip is dropped there; that box is a fallback for a recording with no measured face, and the existing end-to-end pip posts use a higher one.
- **D4. Where it sits, and the caption over it (Amendment 2).** On a real close-up the chin is at about y 1170, and a caption band kept under the card left it about 230 px. The maintainer chose the reference podcast's way, "caption over the card" (translated): the caption is drawn over the lower part of the card. The card's room runs from the face box's bottom (as the zoom and the punches leave it, the face the captions use) plus `zones.gap` down to `zones.caption_bottom`, capped at `card_max_height`; under `card_min_height` the beat is dropped. `position: top` (the default): the card's top is the face's bottom plus `gap`; `position: bottom`: its bottom is `caption_bottom`. A caption chunk that shares frames with a pip gets the band `[card_bottom - caption_min_height, card_bottom]`: never above the card's top, clear of the face because the band is inside a card that lies below it. With a face box bottom at 1188 the card is 354 px tall and drawn.
- **D5. No room: the beat is dropped.** `beats_dropped` keeps its list of ids (the pip's id goes there). A new optional key `beats_dropped_reasons`, a list of `{id, reason}` (at most 100), carries `pip_no_room`; it is written only when it is not empty, so a render with no dropped pip writes the result it always wrote. The HyperFrames engine draws no pip and is untouched.
- **D6. The face check covers the card.** The card's box is marked `data-zone="card"`, so the frame loop's face-box check finds it; `may_cross_face` of `oh:pip` is `false`. A caption chunk that shares frames with a pip gets its band from the card (`_layout.pip_cards`), and the frame loop checks the caption against it (a band inside the card).
- **D7. A hard cut.** The card, its shadow and its label are there on the beat's first frame and last frame: no fade, no slide, no ring. The storyboard's stand-in is a flat-colour card at the computed box (its call does not drop a pip: it draws every beat).
- **D8. Clips.** The ffmpeg command composes a clip in the same box (`kind: "clip"` in a plan row, started at the beat's first frame). The asset loader still refuses a clip (`asset_type`), as before; a clip reaches the renderer only when a later package lets it through, and `pip` tests compose one at the command level.

- **D9. The card is a stream the length of the output, paired by timestamp like the page layers.** On a real phone recording the card blinked and then sat one frame early, while the page layers were exact. The overlay's `enable` was evaluated on a float `t` (a frame number times 1/30 can fall just under the bound it should meet, and `between` is inclusive) and then on the frame number `n`, which the overlay counts in its own bookkeeping; neither was the timestamp the page layers are paired by. The card is now a stream of exactly `out_frames` frames (the asset looped, or a clip started at the beat's first frame) with an alpha mask that is zero outside `start_frame..end_frame-1` (the mask's `geq` reads its own frame number `N`), and the overlay pairs it with the base by timestamp with no `enable`, as it pairs the beat and caption frames. A sparse stream that exists only during the beat was tried first and dropped its last frame when the base's timestamps jittered. Tests: a generated clip with jittered timestamps (five cases, which failed on four before the first fix), and a generated phone-like source (about 60 variable frames a second, a 1/600 time base, audio, a first kept range from frame 55) in which the card and a drawn layer are on the same frames, exactly `start..end-1`. The phone-like case passed before this change too: the one-frame-early start seen on a real recording was not reproduced here, so this change is by construction, not by a failing test.

### What changed in tests

`golden/compose-pip.json` is regenerated (the plan's pip rows now hold the card box and radius; the graph has no speaker bubble). `test_renderer_media.py` rows follow. `renderer_kit.pipeline` passes `--face-box 240,300,600,480` to `base` so the existing pip posts keep their pip. The pinned hashes of the payload functions and of the payload rows, and the `oh:pip` page hash, move with the new page and payload.

### Not verified

No real recording, picture or live creator was used. The look of the card on real footage is for the supervisor's live leg (the synthetic kit post with a landscape, a square and a vertical pip), then the maintainer's.

## 2026-11-07: the media step kind and the new-video flow (V1b1-4; no measurement change, no skill changed, no lab run)

- The step kind `media` (`runtime/step_media.py`) runs VD10's order: take (the post's platform from its `- Network:` line, which must have a video entry, and a script), transcribe, analyse, a still, the proposed retakes and alignment, the caption review (a stop), remap, base, align, check, render, cover, verify, the delivery document, and the `deliver` gate, whose redo is `remap`. It is not unattended.
- The kind imports no provider: the engine (`transcriber:speech`, `renderer:video`, a closed list of verbs) and the store are seams of `runtime/ops_step_seams.py` (`MediaEngine`, `MediaStore`). The engine is the resolver's class default; the key `media.renderer` comes later (V4).
- The way back (G1): while the video waits at the delivery approval, `runtime/ops_media_edit.py` `edit` writes the review and answers the open delivery by code under the decision's lock; the next start renders again, which gives a new hash and a new approval; an edit that changes nothing is refused before anything is answered. After the answer the store makes the task `ready` (not `waiting`). The command and the route that call it are the next package's (V1b2).
- Asset slots (a picture placed in the video) are not in this package: every empty slot is a keyword or none.
- `flows/new-video.json` starts it with the recording's id and the post.
- Spec corrections made while building: the post path is checked by `publish_document.content_key` itself (one source of the rule: a name of at most 80 characters, and never a `.comments` file); the cover file is named by the hash of its own bytes (`MediaStore.settle_cover`), because the delivery document requires `cover-<its sha256[:12]>.png`; the review's proposals (`proposal.edl.json`, `proposal.beats.json`) are what `edit` reads, because they exist at the review and persist through the gate's redo; `step_media.py` also imports `publish_document` (pure), so its row of `ALLOWED` names it.
- The default transcription model is the one constant `step_media.DEFAULT_TRANSCRIBER_MODEL` (`small`, pinned in `providers/transcriber/models.json`; the maintainer's word on it is pending), and the default language is `pt` (`DEFAULT_LANGUAGE`). The project's `media` settings key (`media.language`, `media.transcriber_model`) is read by `MediaStore.setting` but `runtime/project_config.py` does not load it yet.
- Not done here: the `media` settings key of `project_config.py`; the operations, routes, phrases and the expectation ledger of V1b2 (`media-in`, `captions-read`, `captions-edit`, the download); the preview and the messaging path (V1d).

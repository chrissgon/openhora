## 2026-10-28: A greeting in question form is never the OK of a plan, and the last message is read again before a word acts (L64H; no model call, no provider call, no lab run, fingerprint unchanged)

Decision of the maintainer, 2026-10-26 (translated): a greeting written as a question ("oi, tudo bem?", "tudo bem?") is not a yes to the "Posso?" (Shall I go ahead?); "beleza", "tudo bem" and "ok, beleza" stay valid as yes. Open points 1 to 3 of the package were approved with the recommended answers on 2026-10-26.

- **Gap A (L65).** With a plan's question as the last message, "oi, tudo bem?" and "tudo bem?" ran the read: the kind `plan-confirm` asked `natural.confirm_kind` only, "oi" is a courtesy and "tudo bem" is a word of the pt-BR yes list, and `normalise` drops the question mark. **Gap B (L64).** `Bridge.word_act` never read `last_sent` again: a job thread that delivered a reminder between the kind's read and the act left the card word deciding the card (the hash still bound the content). **Gap C.** `plan_decide` read the live plan (`ops.plan_live`) before it started the confirmed call, and a message delivered in that read was not seen.
- **The rule and its place.** `greeting.as_question(line)`: the WHOLE line is a greeting (never a help form) and carries a question mark anywhere in it. It is read in `messaging_turn.plan_confirm_applies`, the kind `plan-confirm`, and nowhere else: changing `natural.confirm_kind` or `affirm` would also have moved the reminders' questions (the expired question's yes, the forget and confirm questions of a draft), the write questions (the e-mail draft's yes or no, and a one-line slot value that is a yes), the interpreter (the closed answer of a frame item and the check of a model's `answer`, which moves its measured numbers) and `plan-bare-word` of the operations layer, with no decision behind any of them. Lines with no question mark, or not wholly a greeting ("oi, tudo bem", "ok, tudo bem?", "tudo bem? sim"), stay a yes (OPEN-L64H-1); English "all good?" is neither a greeting nor a yes word and no word list changes (OPEN-L64H-2). After the greeting the plan is dropped by the rule that drops it for every line that is not its answer, so a later "sim" gets `PLAN_NOT_LAST` and the person asks again (OPEN-L64H-3).
- **The barrier.** `Bridge.still_last(chat, last)` reads `last_sent` under `send_lock`. `word_act` and, for a yes with no reply, `plan_decide` call it as the last read before they act (`last` is the id the word was read against; a reply and a button pass none: they name their message). A message sent since decides nothing: the card is sent once more (`resend`, so the next word decides it; `REPLY_TO_CARD` if it cannot be sent), the plan's yes gets `PLAN_NOT_LAST` and the plan waits. "todos nao" on the list checks once before its loop, because each reject replies and that reply is itself the last message. **Residual window, stated:** `send_lock` is not re-entrant and `act` sends through `deliver`, so the check cannot be held across the act; a send that starts between the check and the operation is not seen (the same model as `reply_alone`; the hash binds the content).
- **The golden.** Two rows changed, `opscomp.plan_last_and_a_greeting_that_is_a_yes.03` and `.09`: their outcome is now the golden outcome of their `plan_not_last` twins (trace `say`, `say-queue`, `greeting`; the greeting summary instead of the calendar sentence; the job `say` instead of `items`). They were replaced with the new mode `entry_corpus_build.py --re-record ID[,ID...] --cause TEXT`, which needs the guard phrase of `--record`, replaces the named rows only, keeps every other line byte for byte and names commit, ids and cause in the header's `changes`. Every other of the 1,089 rows is identical.

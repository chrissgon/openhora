## 2026-10-28: the corpus is replayed once with five observers, the golden in 8 shards, the longest frame test in two (SLOW-1; the maintainer's go, 2026-10-28; OPEN-SL-2 accepted, OPEN-SL-4 decided as SL-4: the golden stays apart)

Five tests replayed the whole entry corpus (about 1,089 scenarios, now the base and every package of `entry_ext/`), and a sixth replayed 188 of them: the golden comparison, `code_claims` against its frozen expression (`test_ops_turn.py`), the operations trace (`test_ops_turn.py`), the bridge trace (`test_turn_order.py`), the reads (`test_turn_gate_refresh.py`) and the envelope (`test_frame_dispatch.py`). The four that are not the golden only observe: each wraps a function, calls the real one and returns its result, and no two wrap the same attribute (`ops_turn.code_claims`; `ops_turn._walk` and `ops_turn.TRACE`; `messaging_turn.TRACE`; `ops_sources.source_refresh` and `turn_gate.may_refresh`; `Ledger.by_message` and `Bridge._handle`). In CI they cost 1,093 s of worker time per run (claims 289 s, ops trace 262 s, reads 274 s, bridge trace 204 s, envelope 64 s), and the golden 282 s (run 40631516525).

- **One observed replay.** `runtime/tests/test_entry_observed.py`: one test in 8 shards (scenario `i` is in shard `i` modulo 8) replays each scenario once with the five observers installed in this order: claims, operations trace, bridge trace, reads, envelope. Each observer and each comparison stays in the file that owns it, as a function the new test imports: `recording_claims` and `claims_problems`, `ops_trace_hook` and `ops_trace_problems` (`test_ops_turn.py`); `bridge_trace_hook` and `bridge_trace_problems` (`test_turn_order.py`); `reads_problems` over the existing `Reads` (`test_turn_gate_refresh.py`); `envelope_hook` (from `reads_of`, which stays, built on it, for the vacuity without the memo), `envelope_problems` and `test_the_envelope_scenarios_are_enough` (`test_frame_dispatch.py`; the whole-corpus counts of the envelope, 100 scenarios and 30 replies, no longer need a replay). Each `*_problems` holds the old comparison word for word, one text for each problem, and the coverage assertions of the old tests (`pairs` not empty, both answers, shells `messaging` and `page`; the dispatch seen: `dispatched > direct`; `worst >= 1`) per shard of 8 instead of 4. The observed test joins the problems under the label of their owner and runs every check even when another found a problem. The removed tests: `test_code_claims_equals_the_old_expression`, the two `test_the_table_trace_equals_the_wrapped_trace` (one in each of `test_ops_turn.py` and `test_turn_order.py`), `test_the_corpus_reads_each_source_at_most_once_per_line` and `test_the_envelope_is_read_once_no_message_is_read_twice_in_a_dispatch`; each file's module text says the check is run once per scenario by `test_entry_observed.py`. One change of the observers: the function a hook wraps is read when the hook runs, not when it is built (`recording_claims`, `envelope_hook`), so that a defect planted before the observers (a test) is what they observe; the old tests built them with nothing planted, so nothing they checked changed. `test_turn_gate_refresh.py` and `test_turn_order.py` lose imports and constants nothing uses (`SHARDS`, and in the first `entry_kit` and `test_entry_replay`).
- **Added beyond the spec: the outcome with the observers is compared with the golden** in every shard of the observed replay (label `an observer changed the outcome`). It costs nothing (the outcome is already computed) and puts the transparency of the observers on every scenario, each run, and not on six. A golden regression therefore also fails the 8 observed shards, with that label last.

- **Proof that the observers do not disturb each other, on the whole corpus (the one-off, not committed).** A throwaway test, through `scripts/test_slots.py` at 4 workers (445 s wall, the machine at a load average of about 12), replayed every scenario of the corpus once with the five observers and once with each alone: 1,089 scenarios, 6 replays each, 6,534 replays. Composed outcome equal to the golden: 1,089 of 1,089. For each observer, its record alone equal to its record in the composed run, and the outcome with it alone equal to the golden: 1,089 of 1,089, for claims, ops trace, bridge trace, reads and envelope alike. Scenarios on which the observer recorded something: claims 545, ops trace 561, bridge trace 902, envelope 131, reads 0. **The reads observer sees nothing in the corpus** (the corpus holds no stale source, as `test_turn_gate_refresh.py` already said: it proves that nothing in it breaks the budget, and the reads themselves are the scripted tests), so for it the transparency is that it changes no outcome and records an empty list; the spec's "a stale-list line that reads a source" and "a mail lookup" are not in the corpus. Nothing was found that makes an observer stay a test of its own.
- **Kept by tests.** `test_the_observers_do_not_see_each_other`: six scenarios (a reply to a card, a page line with claims calls, an operations dispatch, a card word, a line the bridge says, a line on the model's route); each observer alone records what it records composed, the composed outcome is the outcome with no observer and the golden's, and four of the five record something. Vacuity, each run once with its guard removed, the failing tests named in the pull request: each `*_problems` function bites one planted row among accepted ones, and exactly one problem (`test_vacuity_each_check_reports_one_problem_for_one_planted_defect`); a defect planted before the observers on real scenarios is reported by `observed_problems` under its owner (a negated `code_claims`; the row `greeting` of the stage `ops` taking nothing; the bridge row `say-natural` taking nothing, whose effect is the row `say`'s, so only the trace sees it; the memo of the envelope gone; a provider read made before any turn is open), with the same scenarios unplanted as control; a hook planted for the composed run only is seen by the transparency test; a partition that drops a shard, repeats a scenario or changes the interleaving is seen. No cache stands between a patch and a result: each shard computes its own scenarios.
- **Flakes found on the way.** (1) `test_messaging_research_brake.py::test_vacuity_a_stop_that_ignores_whose_run_it_is_...` failed now and then with "the search began after the OK". Cause, the family of FLAKY-RESEARCH-PRESTATE-2: the fixture set the acknowledgement delay to 0.2 s for every line, so a loaded machine let the question's line outlast it, the acknowledgement reached the chat after the question, and the yes was no longer the answer to the last message. Change, test-only: the fixture sets no acknowledgement (3600 s); `Search.ack_soon()` sets 0.2 s before the yes, where the acknowledgement is waited for; the held search and the wait for its start use a bound of 120 s instead of `WAIT` (10 s), which a loaded machine reached before the stop. Proof: a slow first line (0.3 s) and a slow send (0.5 s) pass with the change, and the same slowness with the acknowledgement delay set before the question leaves the yes with no search (the vacuity case). (2) `test_interface_models.py::test_a_model_that_needs_the_consent_...` failed with "refreshed 0 == 1". Cause, from reading and proved: the consent button hashes the warning with `crypto.subtle.digest`, which answers from a thread pool, and the test waited for the result with `settle()`, eight turns of the event loop; on a loaded machine the hash was not back, so `giveConsent` and `refresh` had not been called. Change, test-only: wait until the click's own effect (`calls.refresh >= 1`, then the failure text), with a deadline. Proof: the test runs twice, with the hash at once and 150 ms late; the old test fails on the second with the CI's own message, the new one passes both.
- **Principle 13.** Saved: the four non-golden replays and the envelope pass (1,093 s of worker time per CI run) become one observed replay of about the golden's cost (the spec estimated 310 s with the observers; the measured CI walls are in the pull request); about 780 worker-seconds per run, and the frame test's 68 s item becomes two of about 23 s.

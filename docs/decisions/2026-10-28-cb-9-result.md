## 2026-10-28: the result of the CB-9 measurements and the maintainer's decision to flip the catalog (CB-9; the file `2026-10-28-cb-9-catalog-flip.md` said "pending the measurement" and is not edited; no model call, no provider call, no lab run in this entry, fingerprint unchanged)

This entry publishes what `docs/decisions/2026-10-28-cb-9-catalog-flip.md` left pending: two sittings of the one measurement (the maintainer's, with the plan folder's `ix-cb9.sh`), the raw figures of the harness and the figures read by hand, side by side (principle 10), and his decision. Nothing here was re-run: the raw figures come from the recorded events, and the second report was reproduced for this entry at 0 calls (`ix-cb9-report.py` against an export of head `15101ea8`, the sitting folder read only; the output matches the one the hand reading used).

**How to read the two kinds of figure.** Raw is the harness's own scoring (`interpreter_score`, worst of 2 runs per item, per language: correct_direct at least 0.85, wrong_card point rate at most 0.03, clarify rate at most 0.15, the bars of `evals/interpreter/thresholds.json`). Hand-read ("H") is OPEN-CB9-6 as the maintainer decided it: only the flagged `wrong_card` items are re-scored as the product would act on the model's answer, each traced by calling the real functions of the head with the recorded model texts (no entry replay, no provider read). The hand reading is one-sided by construction (it can only lower a `wrong_card`), which each analysis states and bounds with a sensitivity.

Why it failed, and what round 2 fixed (each at 0 model calls except the re-worded texts):
- **R1, the closed write form pre-empted `repeat`.** `natural_writes` and the bridge claimed repeating lines (7 of the 17 repeat lines of the two sets) and answered "an event that repeats is not available yet", so the flip's `repeat` slot was never reached. Fixed: at catalog version 2, and only when the interpreter is on for that sender and language, a repeating line is not claimed (`natural.repeat_goes_on`).
- **R2, `event_series.reply` lost series words that the model put inside the target** ("weekly", "every day", "this week's"), which turned a series cancel into a question. Fixed: `recurrence.markers` keeps them; the words of the event's own title still never count.
- **R3, gold errors in the time spans.** The bare clock ("6pm", "18h", "17h") was missing on three daily lines. Corrected in the dev set for two of them; the spent blind item `test-pt-br-80011` is published both ways (as scored, a wrong card on Sonnet in both runs; with "17h" accepted, correct) and never rewritten.
- **R4 and R5, model and overlay.** DeepSeek read a repeating calendar line as `routine-create` (no card is shown: the product answers with a refusal sentence), and read a mailbox name as a word filter for `items` (the one real wrong read: a read, no effect). Fixed: `ops_items.mailbox_target` (a mailbox noun is a source or a question, never a filter) and re-worded model-facing texts for `file-ask`, `file-read`, `ask`, `routine-create` and `event-create` (the catalog moved to `160f0831...`).
- The correct_direct misses of the blind set that were not about repeat were hedges between `file-ask`, `ask` and `file-read` on both routes (4 of the 6 non-repeat N2 misses), each safe (a question) and none a gold or scorer error.

**The blind v2 reply-sender gold errors, published both ways.** Items test-en-70015, test-pt-br-70027 and test-pt-br-70029 fail on both routes in both runs: both models drop the mail noun and the "from" lead and answer with "<name> about <subject>" as the target, and the gold span lists hold only the forms that carry the noun or the lead (the two scored-correct siblings, 70016 and 70028, do list the bare form). The product's seed for the mail search (`mail_target_seed`) is identical for the model's target and for every gold target of each item (70015: marcus, ellery, denver, contract; 70027: three content words of a sender and a subject; 70029: joana, mello and a subject word), the sender-grounding rules are not triggered by either form, and `mail_step` returns a card for both: the same search, the same reply card, the reply going to the index's From. So these are gold errors, not wrong reply targets: as scored the group is 2 of 5 correct and 3 of 6 wrong cards; read by hand it is 5 of 5 and 0. The set is not rewritten. **Rule 5 on round 2, read by hand.** The raw wrong reads are `items` lines whose old gold is `items {}` where the v2 model fills `day` (once also `target`); each was checked with `ops_items.items_args` against the line alone. **What was not verified.** No entry replay was run for the flagged lines and no provider read was made: each trace calls the real functions with the recorded texts. The series paths assume a plain series that the person organizes with one instance in the asked range; `_by_words` was not run on a real index (the verdict rests on identical seeds); the refusal wording was not checked.

### The sets are spent

`cb9-blind-v1` (`cb9-blind-split.json`, sha256 `1c9808b7...`) was measured once, in round 1. `cb9-blind-v2` (`cb9-blind-v2-split.json`, set sha256 `10f3308f...`) was measured once, in round 2. Both are spent: never tuned against again, and any later measurement of the catalog needs a new blind set. The dev set `dev-catalog-v1.jsonl` is dev evidence, not blind. Follow-ups that do not touch the measured head and are not part of this decision: add the bare "name about subject" span to the three reply-sender golds in a corrected copy of the spent set, published both ways; make EV_ASK_DAY less poor for a day span that names a week, a plural weekday and a series phrase written as a day (each with a unit test); and a model reading of a monthly event as `routine-create` and of a named-file question as `ask` need a new prompt and a new measurement.

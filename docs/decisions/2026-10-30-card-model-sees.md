## 2026-10-30: CARD-MODEL-SEES: on an open event card the model sees the card (0 model calls by the executor; a draft that merges only after the maintainer's paid measurement; fingerprint unchanged)

**The rule.** The maintainer's decision of 2026-10-30, "O modelo ve o card": a person's request is read by the model, and code rules stay only where the input is fully predictable; hard limits stay in code (principle 11). A closed code form for the title is withdrawn. The line sent after the card of an event that waits is read by the interpreter's own call on the same daily route, with a second fixed text added to its prompt (`runtime/event_card_prompt.txt`) and a sixth line in the user turn, `card:`, before `line:`. The card shows the model five fields (`event_card.view`, a whitelist): `title`, `place` (absent when the card has none), `start`, `end` and the `weekday` of the start. Never the account, the calendar, the event's id or version, the notes, the reminder, the people, the channel or the sender.

**The choice (SPEC-CARD-MODEL-SEES section 1).** Three ways were weighed: a `frame` argument (moves the main prompt, the catalog and `frame.schema.json`: every band and the entry's four hashes), the mail reviser's own prompt (two calls on a line that is another request), and the interpreter's prompt plus a fixed addendum plus a `card:` line (chosen). The interpreter's prompt, the catalog, the protocol and the route are byte equal, so the entry's four hashes, `python3 evals/interpreter_status.py` and every band stay as they are; only `event_card_prompt.txt` is new. The addendum is pinned by `event_card_prompt_sha256` in the newest entry of `interpreter_enable.json`, as the mail prompts are: a changed text turns only the change of an event card off and leaves the interpreter on.

**What code does with the answer (limits L55, L61, L63).** `interpreter.check` for every operation, so every span of any other operation is a piece of the person's line and nothing of the card can become an argument (a title that holds an instruction and an address opens no mail card: tested). A new title or place is a piece of the line (`line-span`), or holds only words of the line and of the card's own title or place, with a word of the line added or taken out (`card-words`, the new origin of the gate). A new end is the model's count and needs the words of the line that say it, after the start and within 24 hours (`time-model`). A day with no hour is read by the time engine and keeps the card's hour. The time checks are unchanged. The kind of effect, the operation, the calendar, the notes and the reminder are copied by code and checked equal; the account changes only by the account word of a card that creates an event. The revised card is a new document the person approves by its own hash; the old card is ended and never approvable (L55). "Another event" is now the model's `target` (words outside the card's title); the closed reading `names_another` stays only where code alone read the time.

**Found while building, fixed here.** In the reading of a day with no hour (`_day_only`) the spec's text kept only the `time` span and dropped the model's `target`: "adia a reuniao do Joao para sexta" would have moved THIS card. **What leaves the host.** On a line that today sends only the line, four fields of one card go to the daily route: mostly already sent (a card made from a line had those words in that line); a change card comes from a snapshot only for an event the person organizes with no other attendees. The route, the admission, the rate, the day, the breaker, the 6 s and the 300 tokens are the interpreter's; no fallback (the card never reaches the second company); no extra call. Retention: the daily route's, shown when `interpret` is widened. Nothing is logged; a change stores nothing, a line that goes on stores the neutral word or the line, no card field.

**Not changed, on purpose.** "adia uma hora" and "uma hora mais tarde" in pt-BR and en are still asked (the time engine flags the words and believing the model's count there would loosen TIME-GUARD); the same lines in es, fr, de and it take the model's count, shown on the card. Taking a place out ("tira o local") is not in this change. A bare time on an open event card ("vai ate as 17h") reaches the model that sees the card instead of the reminder reading (`ops_card_revise._recognised(event=True)`). No word gate before sending the card (the maintainer's rule: "fica com o Iara" has no word).

**Review of PR 680 (2026-10-31), two rounds, changes that answer it.** (1) **A reminder on a waiting card (second round, reversing the first).** L51 was amended on 2026-10-28 by the maintainer: a reminder in words is read by the interpreter on the daily route for a sender who holds `interpret`, like any other line of that sender; only the neutral word of the conversation and the log and store rules stay. No text of L51 changes here. So, while the card of an event waits, a line that `turn_gate.new_request` reads as `bare`, `reminder` or `write` ("tomar remedio as 22h", "melhor as 17h", "starts at 5pm", "cria tambem consulta sexta 15h") reaches the card call too, and goes with the card's four fields (title, place, start, end) to the same route; a typed command (a card word, a decision said in words, a closed form, a question about the state, a greeting, a slash command) does not. The model sees the card and decides: a change makes a new card; anything else goes on to the path the line would have taken (`ops_event_revise.left_to_code`): an operation the model names is kept for the turn that follows, as for any line; a `route`, a `none`, an `answer` or a clarify that only says the model did not read it (a private line the model routes comes back as `private-route`) ends as `{"pass": True}`, unread and unremembered, so the reminder path takes the line and nothing is lost and nothing is said of it; a clarify about a time stays asked. A refusal on such a line is the true refusal, not "what to change?" (Part A's sentence is for a line code does not own). The specification's section 4 ("L51: reminder words are in no card, so nothing moves") does not hold: a reminder's words are now in no card, but its line goes to the card call with the card in view. The first round's closed list of end and length words (`_closed_end`) is removed: it dropped ordinary changes ("as 17h por favor", "17h obrigado", "melhor as 17h", "passa pra 17h", "coloca as 17h", "starts at 5pm", "5pm please"). (2) A day alone on a line the model says is about another event is asked, never carried at this card's hour. (3) The brand of a service inside a new title or place is no account word. (4) The model's `day` slot, when it is not the card's date, leaves the card alone. (5) `_grounded` needs every word taken out to be a word the line says, and one added or removed word that is not a small word; a span of small words alone is no title, place or length. (6) A bare clock with no am or pm that the engine and the model read exactly 12 hours apart takes the model's reading, which saw the card (a probe of a set the executor had not seen found "muda para as 9h" moving a card of 19:00 to 09:00). **Second round:** the rule applies only when the engine's hour is 1 to 12 and the span is a closed bare-clock form (`BARE_CLOCK`: no am, no pm, no part of the day, no word for the hour, no leading zero); "move it to 9am", "as 21h", "as 15h", "9pm", "9h da noite", "as 09:00", "meia-noite" and "meio-dia" keep the engine's reading and `time_disagree` stays true.

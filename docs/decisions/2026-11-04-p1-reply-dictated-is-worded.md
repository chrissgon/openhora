## 2026-11-04: a reply reported after a say verb is worded by the composer, on the maintainer's answer to OPEN-1 (P1 of SPEC-ACEITE-FIX-READING; no model call, no lab run, no prompt or catalog change by this entry)

**The decision.** The acceptance run of 2026-11-04 showed that "responde o email do reembolso dizendo que confirmo e que o valor cai na sexta" and "salva um rascunho respondendo ... dizendo que vou ver amanha" put the literal fragment on the card (rows 6 and 10), while a new e-mail with the same "dizendo que" is worded (row 8). BL7-2 had kept anything after a say verb literal (rule 3 of `mail_reply_compose.described`).
The maintainer answered OPEN-1 on 2026-11-04: yes, a reply dictated after "dizendo que" is worded by the composer; a colon, quotes or "texto exato:" stay literal.

**What changes.** `mail_reply_compose.described` returns True for a body reported after a say verb (the verb is the first word, the word before the body, or one of the up to three words before a complementizer that is before or opens the body). Rules 1 and 2 are checked first and still keep the person's words: the literal marker, a colon or an opening quote right before the body, a body that starts with a quote, and now also a body that starts with a say verb followed by a colon or an opening quote ("saying: yes, friday works"; the span may carry the verb). The compose route, its pinned prompt, its limits and the checks of its answer are unchanged: the model still sees only the date, the language, an empty recipient line, an empty subject line and the person's own words (limits L60 and L63); a refused or failed answer falls back to the person's words with `MC_REPLY_NOT_WORDED`.

**Alternatives rejected.** Keeping rule 3 literal (the row stays a bug in the person's eyes: the new e-mail and the reply read the same words differently). Wording a colon or quoted body too (a person who writes the text, and says so with a colon, quotes or the marker, must get exactly that text).

**What does not change.** No prompt, no catalog, no pinned text, no enabling entry; the golden replay (`runtime/tests/test_entry_replay.py`) moved no row. Tests: `runtime/tests/test_mail_reply_compose.py` (the rows that were False for a say verb are True; the corpus test lets only lines holding a say verb be worded), `test_mail_reply_compose_flow.py` (one compose call for a reply and for a reply draft after "dizendo que", the composed text on the card; a colon or quotes make no call).

## 2026-11-26: Pictures and clips are scanned for text and QR codes before approval, as warnings (P9-a; plan video quality v6, R3-12; media conditions rule item 7; spec `SPEC-video-P9a-scan-before-approval-2026-11-26.md`)

**Status.** DRAFT for the maintainer. P9-a1 (the scanner in the renderer image) merged as #852 (`docs/decisions/2026-11-26-p9a1-scanner-image.md`). This file records the whole package P9-a, built by P9-a1 and P9-a2; P9-a3 (the explaining skill) is not built and waits for P9-3. P9-5 (the page's look and this text) is not answered.

**Context.** The media conditions rule (plan v6 section 3) lets any picture or clip source in under seven conditions; item 7 is a scan by P9. Until P9-a, no new picture or clip source could be enabled. The maintainer chose Tesseract and OpenCV (D8), and his standing rule of 2026-11-24 limits code to security and validity: the scan warns and never refuses.

**Decision.**
- D1. A renderer verb `scan`, in the common image (P9-4), reads every picture and every frame of every clip of a media set for QR codes, and every picture and the frames of a clip it chooses (changed frames, a grid, at most 100 per clip) for text, in a container with no network, on the normalised files only. Tesseract reads English, Portuguese and Spanish (P9-2).
- D2. It runs in `video-media-fetch-picks`, after the normalisation and the adoption of the files; `video-media-approve` shows its rows before the approval, and the box of one open-web file (`video-media-approve-asset`) shows that file's rows. The approval is never refused for a warning, and a scan that failed or was not run is shown as "not checked" with a closed reason.
- D3. The rows are closed codes (`text_found`, `qr_link`, `qr_action`, `qr_text`, `qr_unreadable`, `scan_partial`, `scan_failed`) with phrase sentences built from a slot id, a host, a scheme word and numbers only; the text read and a QR code's text travel only in a `quote` field, which the page draws as a quotation of text lines marked "as read by the computer, not checked" (limit L82). The terminal prints the same answer.
- D4. The result is kept in `scan.json` beside the set, keyed by file hash (a re-fetch that keeps a file scans only the new ones), never served and not a name a step may read; the set document, its hash and the media step's reading of `approved.json` are unchanged, so no earlier approval goes stale; `approved.json` gains the additive key `scan`, the digest of what was shown.
- D5. Nothing leaves the computer in P9-a1 and P9-a2. P9-a3, the skill that would explain the text found, is not built: it waits for P9-3, the maintainer's answer on whether scanned text may be sent to a model and how.
- D6. Licences and sources: P9-1 and the merge answer are recorded in `docs/decisions/2026-11-26-p9a1-scanner-image.md`. Languages: English, Portuguese and Spanish, the maintainer's own answer P9-2, recorded in the same file; the page's scope line says that other alphabets are not read.
- D7. Media conditions rule item 7 is met for pictures and clips from this merge on.

**Built in P9-a2 (differences from the spec, each small).**
- The cache of `scan.json` is keyed by the file's sha256 and the scan language; the version of the scanner image is recorded in the file but a newer image does not make a kept file scan again (a validity saving: the cache only spares a re-read, and a new fetch of a changed file always scans).
- A scan that did not run has one row with no slot (`scan_failed`) and the sentence names the closed reason; a set fetched before P9-a has `state: not_run` and its own sentence (`CARD_VIDEO_SCAN_NOT_RUN`), since no reason applies.
- One row per slot, code and (for a link or an action) host or scheme, so a second QR host is not lost; `scan_partial` says how many frames QR codes were read on, because the verb sets it only when its time budget ran out.
- `ops_step_seams.MEDIA_ENGINE_CLASSES` lists the `scan` verb, and `SheetEngine` keeps a closed reason of the scan (`timeout`, or one of the five closed words from the renderer's refusal line).

**What it saves and what it loses (principle 13).** Saves: a picture with a stock watermark, a phone number or a QR link reaches the person's eyes before it reaches a post. Loses: a bigger common image and one rebuild for everyone (P9-a1); text in other alphabets is not read; text on a clip frame the verb did not choose is not read (QR codes are read on every frame); a kept file is not scanned again by a newer image.

**Verified and not verified.** Verified by offline tests with the fake engine seam: that the scan is called once after the normalisation for every asset of one of each source tier, that each closed reason leaves the fetch and the set whole, that the rows and sentences are built from the closed fields only in both languages, that a planted line asking to be obeyed appears only in `quote` and nowhere else outside `scan.json`, that the approval goes through whatever the rows say, that the set's hash and the media step's hash are unchanged, that an approval made before P9-a still binds the set, that `scan.json` is never served and cleaned on read, and that the page draws the rows and the quotation as text. Not verified: a real scan through the real engine, the page's look at phone and desktop width in light and dark (L3), the Portuguese sentences read by the maintainer, P9-3 and P9-5.

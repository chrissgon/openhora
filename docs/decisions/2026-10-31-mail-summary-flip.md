The reason says "Measured on this PR's head before merge ... the PR merges only after the maintainer's decision on that measurement", as CB-9's did. After the measurement the maintainer (or the supervisor on his word) sets the entry's `date` to the day of his decision and may add the figures to `reason` and `status_line` (600 characters each; `test_interpreter_enable.py` holds the length); nothing else of the entry moves unless a re-worded text changes the catalog hash, in which case the hash, the epoch, the pin and `mail-ask`'s tests move together (`interpreter_catalog.py --write` and the pin are the two places).

Tests: `runtime/tests/test_mail_ask_binding.py` (a planted old condition fails two of them).

## 2026-10-30: the router's run on a request moves to ops_requests, and the recorder of a use to ops_runs (44-C3; SPEC-44C, OPEN-C1 B, OPEN-C3)

**Decision.** The router's run on a request leaves `runtime/ops.py` for the new `runtime/ops_requests.py`: `_routable`, `_route_request`, `_router_call`, `_checked`, `_combined`, `_route_run`, `_not_recognised`, `_item_text`, `_route_deliveries` and `ROUTE_KEPT`. The recorder of a use goes to `ops_runs` (`USE_ID`, `RECORDER_TIMEOUT`, `_recorder`, `_record`, `_use_start`): a run's use is recorded where runs live. The names keep their bodies byte for byte; the only lines that differ from the base are the two qualifiers of `_router_call` (`_route(` is `ops_models.route(`, `_use_start(` is `ops_runs._use_start(`), which the PR lists. `ops.py` keeps re-exporting every moved name after the marker, so `dir(ops)` is the base's plus the module name `ops_requests`.

**What the callers do now.** The operation `route` and the reroute of a delivery after its brief (`_reroute`, which stays in `ops.py` until 44-C4) call `ops_requests.<name>`; the task's run body (`_run_body`) and `verdict` call `ops_runs._use_start` and `ops_runs._record`. With one production binding there is one patch target, so the two patches that reached `ops._use_start` (the golden kit's stub and the test that watches the use of a refused router run) are re-pointed to `ops_runs._use_start`.

**The last `_ops()` of a conversation.** `ops_requests` imports `ops_say` (the person's line, the question of a reply that names no route), so `ops_say` cannot import it back. `_drain_route` reaches `_route_request` with `importlib.import_module("ops_requests")` at call time, a `cycle:` row in `test_import_breakers.py` that replaces the `ops_say -> ops` row; `_ops` is deleted from `ops_say`. What reaches `ops` at call time is now the dispatcher (the entry), the seam and `ops_runs` (the run body and the round, 44-C4): `test_ops_facade.py` pins that set.

**Two small things from the C2 review.** The seam's rule in `test_ops_facade.py` now also rejects a literal that names a module of `ops` (`function("ops_runs")` passed: a public name, a module by value) or something that cannot be called, with a planted case for each; and the dead `import importlib` of `ops_reads.py` is gone.

**Budgets and rows.** `LAZY_CALLS_BUDGET` 58, `LAZY_EDGES_BUDGET` 47, `PLAIN_DEBT_BUDGET` 0 (the file's own AST count; one edge out, one in); `OPS_PY_BUDGET` 2,476 to 2,186 (290 code lines moved; headroom 32 as before). `ops_requests.py` is 319 lines and `ops_runs.py` 1,014, both under the 1,200 of a sibling.

**Alternatives rejected.** Moving `route` itself to `ops_requests` (that is alternative A of OPEN-C1: the operation stays in the facade, a sibling that needs it uses the seam). Keeping `_use_start` in `ops.py` and reaching it from `ops_requests` through the seam (the seam takes public names only, and a helper moves to the sibling that owns its job). Giving `ops_say` a top-level import of `ops_requests` (it closes a cycle).

**Not changed.** No rule, phrase, policy, approval or card hash; no effect path, no limit L1 to L8. The entry golden is untouched and replays identically.

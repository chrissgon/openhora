## 2026-11-07: decision 14, a video from the phone, its preview and its approval on a messaging channel (V1b1-3; no measurement change, no skill changed, no lab run)

- **The maintainer's decision, 2026-11-07:** "Pode mudar a decisao, a ideia de mandar a gravacao pelo celular, receber previa e aprovar é muito boa e um diferencial legal" (validate: allow english-only -- the maintainer's own words, quoted). It changes VD18 of `2026-11-07-v0-short-video-hyperframes.md`, whose "Alternatives rejected" listed "Approval on messaging by grant"; that record is not edited, and this entry is the one that holds from now on.
- **Recording in.** A recording sent by a listed sender on a messaging channel the project granted becomes the input of a new-video task. Ordinary attachments still refuse video, and their cap (the least of 25 MiB and the provider's own) widens only for the media step, by a per-project grant. Built by the messaging package (V1d), not here.
- **Preview out.** The channel receives a preview encoded from the render, small enough for its send cap. The delivery document carries the render's sha256 and the preview's sha256 and the render it was made from, so one approval binds the exact render the preview showed (`runtime/deliver_document.py`, `preview`).
- **Approval.** `approve-effect-deliver` is a messaging grant (`runtime/operations.py` `GRANTS`), given only with `accept-config --widen` in the terminal; the default stays terminal and the loopback page. A messaging card approves a delivery only when it carried the whole preview of that render to that chat: `on_cards` is true only when the document's preview was made from its render, and the bridge's per-chat rule (a card is whole only after the provider confirmed its file) does the rest. Until a channel sends previews, the grant approves nothing, as `approve-effect-create` approves nothing until a card shows the change.
- **Kept from VD18:** an approval that arrives on the page opened over the local network (`--lan`) is refused (`operations.LAN_REFUSED_EFFECTS`, `runtime/service.py`); the MCP mode is granted no effect.
- **The way back** (caption edit, render again, approve again) works from the channel too; the messaging package binds a caption correction to the task.
- **Cost stated.** The recording and the preview pass through the messaging provider's servers; the person accepts that per channel with the grant, whose text says so. Retention (decision 12) applies to the local copies; what the provider keeps is outside the platform.
- **Settled by the maintainer, 2026-11-07:** "Vamos começar pelo telegram. Caso o video da pessoa seja mais pesado do que o canal suporta, diga que o video é muito pesado e a pessoa deve enviar pela plataforma, ou através de alguma nuvem como google drive e etc (Ela pode indicar onde o video está e o agente pega e baixa na plataforma)" (validate: allow english-only -- the maintainer's own words, quoted). Telegram is the first channel. A recording over the channel's cap is answered with a fixed phrase (English and Brazilian Portuguese) that names the two ways in: the upload of the local page, or a cloud location the person names in the chat, Google Drive first. The workbench fetches that recording into the task through `runtime/intake.py`, never through a model, and the recording's own cap for the media step applies to every way in. Built by the messaging package (V1d), not here.
- **Channel caps, stated in code by the messaging package:** Telegram's Bot API download 20 MB and send 20 MiB, Discord 25 MiB, WhatsApp 100 MB (to confirm live), Twilio 16 MB. A recording over the cap gets the fixed phrase above, and no model is involved.

## 2026-11-08: the tier-B ports and the HyperFrames defaults (V3b-4; no measurement change, no skill changed, no lab run)

**What.** Five components of the HyperFrames registry, read at commit `b4cc674f7f312e77ded32e0b53006cc0bad6723b` (tag v0.9.19), are rewritten as our own tier-B
blocks, each one a `gsap.html` of the contract of `_gsap_lint.py` and a `block.json` with `tier: "B"` and nothing else: `hf:caption-highlight` and
`hf:caption-pill-karaoke` (preset `caption`), `hf:count-up` (`number`), `hf:testimonial-card` (`quote`) and `hf:spring-pop` (`keyword`). None has a `renderat`
page, so none draws on the ffmpeg engine (`block_not_on_engine`). Each declares exactly its preset's parameters and draws every one of them. The final
`blocks/engines/hyperframes.json` runs `gsap` and `renderat`; its defaults are `caption -> hf:caption-highlight`, `keyword -> hf:spring-pop`, `number ->
hf:count-up`, `quote -> hf:testimonial-card` and `list -> oh:list`; `cutaway` and `pip` stay out. `hyperframes-image/NOTICE.md` has one entry per port and
`hyperframes-image/LICENSE-registry-Apache-2.0.txt` is the registry's licence file byte for byte (sha256 `4259155f...36b8`; a test holds it). `pins.json` and
`catalogue.json` are written by `pin-blocks --confirmed`.

**Why.** Plan row V3b (tier B, the ports, NOTICE, C10); V3b spec section 8 and OPEN-3 to OPEN-7, OPEN-13. S2 measured that the registry's caption styles
put their lowest glyph at 1586 and 1580 px on the real face (366 and 289 frames below 1560), so a port that keeps the source's positions is refused by the gate
of V3b-2. The ports are therefore placed by the band: the stage fills the instance (which is the caption band, `_layout.zones`), the text is anchored to its
bottom with a padding of 44 px, the line height is 1.25 (the glyph rectangle of Inter, 1.21 em, sits inside the line box), no caption word is scaled
beyond 1.03 and none starts lower than its place, and the type is shrunk by arithmetic until the lines, the padding and the pill fit the band's height. The
type is sized from the number of characters and an em-width constant, never by measuring a canvas: the page's `build` runs once at load, when a web font may
not be loaded yet, and `document` is not available to a fragment (`_gsap_lint`). `caption-kinetic-slam` is not ported (50 face hits in S2).

**Settled by the supervisor on the spec's recommendation (2026-11-07), not the maintainer's word.** OPEN-3 (no `cutaway` and no `pip` on this engine), OPEN-4
(5.9 passed, so `list` stays `oh:list` on `renderat`; no `gsap.html` for it), OPEN-5 (the defaults above, after the maintainer's C10 approval), OPEN-6 (the
`notice` field is a pointer in our words, `Apache-2.0; adapted from the HyperFrames registry at b4cc674f; notices in providers/renderer/hyperframes-image/NOTICE.md`,
with no maker's name; the copyright line lives in NOTICE.md and in the licence file), OPEN-7 (tier-A captions refused; captions only as these ports), OPEN-13
(NOTICE and the licence text in the image folder, not under `blocks/`), OPEN-11 (contact sheets local, not committed).

**Choices where the spec left room.**

- **Colours are theme tokens, not the source's literals.** The source styles use a red sweep with white text (highlight), a light pill with grey and black
  words (pill karaoke), a green accent (count-up) and slate surfaces. The ports use the theme's own tokens, so the look follows the edit list's theme: highlight
  = `--c-accent` behind the word with `--c-bg` text while it is said; pill = `--c-text` pill, `--c-muted` words turning `--c-bg`; the cards use `--c-chip_fill`,
  `--c-chip_border`, `--c-text` and the `color` parameter (a palette token, as on the `oh:` blocks). Literals used: a `rgba(0, 0, 0, a)` text shadow (all five),
  a `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.18)` pill shadow, `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.45)` and `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.35)` card shadows. No other literal colour. This is a departure from "ports keep
  their look"; the C10 sheet is where the maintainer judges it.
- **`testimonial-card` draws no star rating and no handle.** The tier-A dynamic run refused the unported component with `font_not_allowed`: its five U+2605
  stars fall back to a system font, which the HyperFrames image does not have and the font guard refuses. The spike's port hid the rating with CSS. The port
  never writes the characters (a test asserts every port file is ASCII), draws the sentence, an initials avatar and the author, and keeps `color` as the
  card's border and avatar colour. The component's own handle and rating have no parameter in the `quote` preset.
- **`spring-pop` draws the `icon` parameter with our eight icons** (the paths of `oh:keyword`, in the fragment's markup; the fragment cannot create SVG elements
  because `document` is banned); the dot of the source remains when no icon is given. It adds a 0.15 s fade at the end, which the source does not have.
- **`count-up` counts the written value** (`1.234,56` keeps its separators and its last frame is the value exactly, as `oh:number` does) from zero, with the
  source's sine ease, its arrival, its landing pulse of 1.07 and its timing scaled to the beat when the beat is shorter than 2.05 s; it adds a `label` and a
  `color` and has no `start`, `prefix`, `suffix`, `accent`, `glow` or `exit` (the preset has no such parameter).
- **Sizes are pixels, not container units.** The source uses `cqw`/`cqh`/`cqmin`; the instance is a clipped element of the root composition, so the fragment
  sizes from `ctx.band` (1080 wide, the band's height) with the same fit shares the `oh:` pages use (86 % of the band for a card, 84 % for the badge).
- **The caption ports' `anchor`.** `data.anchor` is `bottom` (the default of the edit list) or `center`; `bottom` is bottom-anchored in the band, `center` is
  centred in what is left above the 44 px padding.

**Saving and loss (principle 13).** Sizing by arithmetic instead of measuring the page saves a font-load wait and a dependence on `document` in a fragment, and
costs some fit: the em-width constants are conservative (0.74 em per upper-case letter, 0.66 em per lower-case letter, 0.62 to 0.66 em per digit), so a short
phrase is set a little smaller than the browser could fit it; the gates (the 1560 line, the face box, the pixel check) are what catch a case the arithmetic
misses, and the instance's clip keeps what a card draws inside its band. Using theme tokens instead of the source's literals saves a colour review per theme and
costs the source's exact look, which is the maintainer's judgement at C10. Leaving out the star rating costs a visible feature of one source and removes a
glyph that the font guard refuses.

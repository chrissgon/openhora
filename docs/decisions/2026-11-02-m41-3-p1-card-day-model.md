## 2026-11-02: A day alone, or an hour beside another change, on a card takes the model's start under four checks (M41-3 P1 card-day-model, change A of SPEC-M41-3-OLD-FINDINGS; no prompt change, no model call, no provider call, no lab run, fingerprint unchanged)

**What happened.** In the phone test of 2026-11-02 (row 21) "passa pra quinta que vem" and "passa pras 15h e muda o local pro Bistro Exemplo", sent after the card of an event, were answered with "I am not sure of the day and the time" and the card was sent again. The model's answer was already a well-formed `event-change` with the whole new start (`time_iso`, `weekday`) and the parts; the code asked: `interpreter._classify` asks for a day alone it doubts ("que vem") or does not know ("donnerstag", "next tuesday"), and `_day_only` takes only a plain date. The recorded answers of 2026-11-01 for cb-16, cb-18, cb-27 and cb-28 of the blind set show the same four cases on both routes. validate: allow english-only -- the phone lines, Portuguese

**Decision.** In `ops_event_revise.read_answer`, when the checks ask with exactly `time-ask` and `_day_only` gives nothing, the model's start is taken by the new pure `_model_time`, which returns `{"local": time_iso, "source": "model"}` only when all four checks hold: (1) form: the span is a piece of the line, `time_iso` is a real moment, `weekday` is its weekday, the card's start is a plain `YYYY-MM-DDTHH:MM`; (2) earlier decisions: the line is not a distance from now, not a deadline set apart, and every hit of the time engine on it is a date, a slash date or a clock (so "atrasa meia hr", an offset, stays asked); (3) what code reads clearly is code's: each clock the engine finds reads as the hour of `time_iso` and lies in the span, and a day alone keeps the card's hour; (4) the day the line names: a plain day is that day, a weekday alone is that weekday 1 to 14 days after today (today is asked), an hour alone keeps the card's day. `time-past`, `time-far`, `time-gap`, `time-fold` and `weekday` stay as they are. The guards after it stay: another event as target, a day of another event, `change_of`, `_refused` (origin `time-model`), the new card opened by the typed command's operation and `matches`, approval by its own hash. No prompt, catalog or protocol file changes, so the enable entry of 2026-11-01 (`event_card_prompt_sha256` `fcdec0eb`) stays in force. Tests: `runtime/tests/test_card_day_model.py`. Docs: the module text and the `read_answer` docstring of `ops_event_revise.py`, row 65 of `docs/architecture/platform/messaging.md`.

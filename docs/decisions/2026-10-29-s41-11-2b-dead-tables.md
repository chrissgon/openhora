## 2026-10-29: the dead natural-language readers go, and what stays with its reader (S41-11 PR 2b; no model call, no provider call, no lab run, fingerprint unchanged)

PR 2a took every closed claim of a plain line out of the bridge and the operations layer. This change deletes what 2a left without a reader and keeps, with the name of its reader, everything the spec said to delete that something still reads (the supervisor's rule of 2026-10-29: if something else still reads a thing, keep it and list it).

**Deleted.**
- `natural.needs`: its two callers read the one closed reading code keeps, `natural.decision`. The group a plain line needs is `ops_natural.line_group` (a decision said in words is `pending`, everything else `talk`), and the bridge's own fallback, for a bridge with no project to ask, is `messaging_bridge.line_group`.
- `natural.claim`: no product site called it since 2a; the stand-in of the tests has it (`runtime/tests/old_claim.py`).
- `natural._trigger_span` and the trigger, bare and note branches of `natural.understand`: the way `trigger` of a reading was produced only by the claim of a line. `understand` reads with `command` (the default now) and `write`. `ops_reminders.remind` keeps its `how="trigger"` default untouched: every product caller passes the way, and the tests that use the default enter through `closed_heads.trigger_reading`.
- `scripts/registry_build.py`, its two tests in `test_registry_data.py` (the files equal what the builder makes; the builder is deterministic and has help) and its row in the layer map. The data of `runtime/registry/*.json` is edited by hand under review, as the data file's own header says since S41-9. The layer map also loses the row of `scripts/interpreter_corpus_tier1.py`, which 2a deleted and left in the table.
- Thirteen cases of one test of `test_natural_reminders.py` (the words of the reminder are the line without the trigger) and their data `TEXT_CASES`: their subject, the cut of the trigger words, is gone.

**Kept, with its reader (the spec's rows 2b did not hold).**
- `natural.candidate` with `_forget`, `_undo`, `_verb_noun`: read INSIDE `natural.py` by the reminder question engine, three sites that nothing in the spec listed: `short_answer` (`natural.py:602`: a reply that is itself a request is no attempt at an answer) and two in `complete` (`:726` and `:802`: a line that starts a request of its own is a new line, never the answer to the question). Taking `candidate` away changes what an open reminder question does with "meus lembretes", "desfaz" or "cancela o lembrete 3" typed in answer to it (the question would be asked again, or the words taken as the answer): a behaviour change of the live engine that no golden row decides, so it is not made here.
- Every table key `candidate` and those three helpers read: `triggers.reminders`, `reminders_bare`, `undo`, `note`, `forget.verbs`, `id_words`, `id_marked`, `calendar_noun_reads`, and two the spec did not list, `problem_words` and `remind_nouns`. `triggers.remind` and `remind_anywhere` and `calendar*` have their own live readers (`only_trigger`, `natural_writes.reminder_outside`, `natural_writes_calendar._create`, `interpreter._head_phrases`), as the spec said for the calendar ones; `triggers.remind` is read by `only_trigger` too, which the spec took for dead.
- `natural.match` and `requests.forms` (the supervisor's ruling of 2026-10-29 00:30: `interpreter._classify` keeps `natural.match`; and `decision`, the brake hints of `ops_turn.brake_hint` and `Bridge.brake_form`, and `ops_natural_writes._classify` read it).
- `ops_card_revise._recognised` keeps `natural.candidate`. The re-point the supervisor asked for (`turn_gate.private_kind` and `lead_kind` instead) changed two golden rows (`guard.card_words.27` and `.28`: "Agende corte de cabelo amanha as 14h" with its accents, the loose scheduling verb that only `candidate` calls a request), and the entry golden must replay identical, so the line stays as it was.
- `natural.repeat_scope` and `natural.repeat_goes_on` (CB-9): no product site asks `repeat_goes_on` since `claim` went; the stand-in of the write families reads it (`closed_heads.make`), and its four openers (`ops_say`, `ops_natural_writes`, `ops_interpret.repeat_scope`, the bridge) stay with it. They go together with `candidate`.
- `how` values `bare`, `note` and `ask` in `messaging_remind.natural`, `natural._next`/`question`: reachable only from a draft built by a pre-state or a stand-in.

**What the tests do now.** `closed_heads.py` and `entry_kit.py` use only what exists: `old_claim.py` holds the frozen `claim`, `_trigger_span` and `understand` (byte for byte the old functions, equal to them on 2,966 lines of the corpora for `claim` and `understand`), `closed_heads.claim_forms` puts the old group (`ops_natural.line_group`, `messaging_bridge.line_group`) and the old reading engine back, and `closed_heads.trigger_reading` is the old engine alone, for the eight modules whose tests enter the reminder engine with a line that starts with a remind word (the engine they guard is the same function for every other way). The entry replay patches `natural.understand` with the old function for the whole scenario, because a draft a pre-state builds has the way `trigger`, which the product no longer produces: the golden replays identical (35 passed, 16 skipped, none re-recorded).

**The two twins of the review of 2a (round 2).** `test_vacuity_h1_with_the_old_clause_back_the_raw_line_reaches_the_planning_agent` puts the old `or extras.get("route_pass") is not None` clause back in `typed-private-line` and the first leg's lack of a typed answer, and shows the raw line reaching the planning agent (idle) or kept for its turn (slot held). The vacuity case of `test_closed_heads_gone.py` runs the old reader of `closed_heads.py` on the very lines of `LINES` and shows that it reads each of them where the product reads none.

**T9.** The tests that failed on the 2b head before any edit: 63, in 12 files (13 deleted with their data, 2 builder tests deleted, 3 edited for `needs` → `line_group`, 1 for `claim`, 1 for the trigger half of a write-mode test, 45 re-targeted through `trigger_reading`). The CPU-s of the deleted ones, from one timed run on the base: about 0.3 s. The saving the spec hoped for (about 830 to 1,275 CPU-s) needs `candidate` gone, which waits for the follow-up below. No test of the rows L51, L54, L60 to L65 is cut.

**The follow-up this asks for (the maintainer's call).** What replaces `natural.candidate` in `short_answer` and `complete`. The recommendation: the ops layer asks, before it hands a line to `natural.complete`, whether the line starts a request of its own by `turn_gate.lead_kind` (the two heads of S41-9's data) and `natural.decision`, a measured set of reply lines decides what is lost (the reminder list, the undo and the forget by words, which the interpreter reads), and then `candidate`, the three helpers, the ten keys and `repeat_scope` go with their tests in one package.

## 2026-11-23: The split layout preset: the speaker in one half, the person's picture in the other (V9f; mkt-social-copy 3.2.0, Y)

**Decision.** `split` is added to `shared/references/video-presets.json` and `video-presets.md`: kind `layout`, main `label`, parameters `asset` (image or clip, required), `side` (`top`, `bottom`, `left`, `right`, default `bottom`: the half the picture takes; the speaker takes the other), `fit` (`cover` or `contain`, default `cover`) and `label` (text, at most 40). The vocabulary stays at version 2: both readers pin the versions they accept (`check_script.py` reads 1 and 2, the renderer's `VOCABULARY_VERSIONS` is `(1, 2)`), decision D2 of `2026-11-07-v2-script-vocabulary-v2.md` ties the version to the file's structure, and a preset is additive. The vocabulary stays closed: only `split` is added, no other preset and no other parameter.

**Why.** The maintainer's request: "Short videos need a split-screen layout: the speaker talking in one half of the frame, a picture or clip the person uploads in the other half. Today the closed vocabulary only has two layout presets: `cutaway` (the asset fills the frame and the speaker disappears) and `pip` (the asset sits in a bubble over the speaker). Neither one splits the frame." The backlog item V9E-SPLIT-SCREEN (17 split-screen events in 9 of the labelled reference videos, from `2026-11-11-v9e-pip-card.md`) asked for the same and is deleted: `side: top` is its shape.

**What the renderer does (the ffmpeg engine only).**
- The frame (1080 x 1920) is split at its middle: `top` and `bottom` at y = 960, `left` and `right` at x = 540. The page draws the picture in its half (`object-fit` cover or contain, the theme's background behind it) and leaves the other half transparent.
- ffmpeg lays the speaker in the other half: a crop of the picture it already draws (cut, zoomed, punched), the size of that half, not scaled, centred on the beat's face box and kept inside the frame, masked to the beat's frames. The moved face is the face box shifted by the crop and clipped to the half (`_layout.split_geometry`).
- The captions take a band in the picture's half: the lowest stretch of rows, at least `caption_min_height` tall, that keeps `gap` from the face as it was, from the moved face and, for `top` and `bottom`, from the whole speaker half, so no caption crosses the seam. For `left` and `right` the band is at most `split.bar_height` (180) rows and the page draws an opaque bar of the theme's background over exactly those rows, so the vertical seam never runs under a caption. A caption chunk that shares frames with a split takes its band and its face becomes the union of its face and the moved face.
- A split with no such band is dropped, as a pip with no card is: its id goes to `dropped` and `{id, reason: split_no_room}` to `dropped_reasons`.
- The label chip is in the picture half's corner nearest the seam, inside the safe width.
- HyperFrames draws no `split`: a beat with it is refused `preset_not_on_engine`, as a `pip` is. A clip in a split slot is refused `asset_type`, as in a pip; the page draws images only.

**Alternatives rejected.** A page-only split: the default face box (rows 620 to 1180) would be cut by the seam at y = 960, and only ffmpeg can move the speaker. Changing the shared caption page to take a horizontal box: it is pinned byte for byte and every render draws it. Scaling the speaker to fit a half: it changes the face size and the arithmetic of the face check.

**Assumed (the open questions of the spec, built as recommended).** Q1: the default `side` is `bottom`, as the request states (the backlog's measured shape is `top`, one value away). Q2: a face box wider than half the frame on `left` or `right` is cut at the seam or the frame's edge; the crop is not scaled. Q3: the eval cases of `mkt-social-copy` that list the layouts name `split`. Q4: `left` and `right` captions are protected by an opaque bar, not by a change of the caption page.

**Not changed.** `cutaway`, `pip`, every `hf:` block, the runtime, the interface, `mkt-video-media-search` (its "cutaway" is the generic b-roll sense), the compose goldens and the pinned payload functions (`pip_geometry`, `caption_data`, `beat_data`).

**Spec mismatches found in the code at build time.** The HyperFrames engine now draws `oh:cutaway` (V9b-2, V9b-5b), so its note says "no pip and no split" and `short-video.md` says HyperFrames has no `pip` or `split`; clips are drawn by the ffmpeg engine's compose for the cutaway only, so the split's "not verified" note says a clip in a split slot is `asset_type`.

**Not verified here.** Only the host's headless browser drew the page and only a synthetic recording went through the crop; the look on a real recording, a face box wider than half the frame on a `left` or `right` split and a render in the container are not verified. Lab tests of `mkt-social-copy` 3.2.0 are pending (a Y change: its evidence is inherited, since it cites the vocabulary). The renderer's ffmpeg image tag changes (`renderer-presets.json`, `_layout.py`, `_media.py`, `_frames.py`, `_payload.py`, `_render.py` are in its manifest): the maintainer rebuilds it (`ffmpeg.py build --confirmed`) before a real render.

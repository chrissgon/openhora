## 2026-10-30: a sibling calls an operation of the facade through one seam, and the reads read what moved (44-C2; SPEC-44C, OPEN-C1 B, OPEN-C5)

**Decision.** The maintainer answered OPEN-C1 with B, the recommended single seam. A sibling of `runtime/ops.py` that needs an operation of the table, or a public entry of the facade, calls it as a shell does, by name, through the new `runtime/ops_facade.py`: `ops_facade.function(name)` returns `ops.<name>`, read at the moment of the call and never kept. Only private helpers move. The six siblings that reached `ops` (`ops_natural`, `ops_natural_writes`, `ops_say`, `ops_card_revise`, `ops_event_revise`, `ops_instructions`) call `pending`, `answer`, `reject`, `status`, `progress`, `request`, `route`, `natural_write`, `natural_forget`, `event_create`, `event_change` and the call of each row of the table (`row["call"]`, three dispatches) through it, and their `_ops()` is deleted (`ops_say` keeps it for `_drain_route`, which reads `_route_request`; 44-C3 moves that). `ops_event_revise` read `getattr(ops, name)` with `name` one of `event_create` and `event_change`: it is now `ops_facade.function(name)`, so its edge to `ops` (the last `debt:` edge) is gone.

**What moved (OPEN-C5: `SERVICE` to `ops_reads`).** `SERVICE` and `_held_listed` go to `ops_reads` (`status` calls `ops_reads._held_listed`); `HELD_CURSOR`, `HELD_KEPT`, `_now_iso` and `_record_held` go to `ops_runs` (the round writes the held record, `ops_reads` reads `ops_runs.HELD_CURSOR`). 55 code lines leave `ops.py`; the bodies are byte for byte but for the qualifiers (`ops_reads.held_next` is `held_next` in its own module, `HELD_CURSOR` is `ops_runs.HELD_CURSOR` in `_held_listed`). `ops_reads` stops calling `ops`: `_agents_of_the_day` and `_now_iso` are `ops_runs.<name>`, `ops_steps` is imported at the top, and `service_check` reads `dispatcher.inspect(core)` (`inspect` reads only `__file__`, and `ops_core.py` is in the folder of `ops.py`). `ops_say` reads `ops_models.route`, `ops_runs._midnight_utc` and `ops_runs.PER_RUN_USD` where it read `ops._route`, `ops._midnight_utc` and `ops.PER_RUN_USD`. `ops.py` re-exports `SERVICE` and `_held_listed` (from `ops_reads`) and the four held names (from `ops_runs`) after the marker, so `dir(ops)` is unchanged and `setitem(ops.SERVICE, ...)` still mutates the dict the reads use.

**The rule of the seam.** Every literal argument of `ops_facade.function(...)` is a public name of `ops` (no leading `_`, not UPPER_CASE); a call that passes a variable is one of four (the three dispatches of `row["call"]`, and `name` in `ops_event_revise`), and the module is imported as `import ops_facade`. `runtime/tests/test_ops_facade.py` pins (a) a patch on `ops.<name>` is what `function(name)` returns, (b) the rule, with a planted private name, constant, unlisted variable and other import forms, (c) the set of modules that load `ops` by name. The seam is the one lazy edge to `ops` that a sibling keeps, `CYCLE_FACADE`. It imports nothing of the runtime, so every sibling may import it at the top.

**Why the tests still reach production.** The seam resolves `ops.<name>` at call time, so the sentinel of the L54 walk (`spy_all` in `test_ops_natural.py`), the sentinels of the effect paths (`test_ops_natural_writes.py`), the golden kit's spies on `approve`, `reject`, `release`, `natural_write` and the rest, and every `monkeypatch.setattr(ops, ...)` keep patching what production calls. Seven tests patched `ops_say._ops` with a fake namespace; they patch `ops.status`, `ops.progress`, `ops.request`, `ops.route` and `ops.answer` instead, and each line has its "comment it out and the test fails" check in the PR. The A.6 comparison (the calls each replacement receives, before and after) is in the PR.

**Budgets and rows.** `LAZY_CALLS_BUDGET` 64 to 58, `LAZY_EDGES_BUDGET` 52 to 47, `PLAIN_DEBT_BUDGET` 1 to 0 (the file's own AST count); `OPS_PY_BUDGET` 2,531 to 2,476 (headroom 32 as before). Six rows to `ops` are gone, `("ops_facade", CYCLE_FACADE, "ops")` is new, `DEBT_OPS` is deleted, and `CYCLE_OPS`, which `ops_say` and `ops_runs` keep, names what each still reads (the router's run, 44-C3; the run body and the round, 44-C4, OPEN-C2). After this package the modules that load `ops` by name are `dispatcher` (the entry, OPEN-C4), `ops_facade` (the seam), `ops_say` and `ops_runs`.

**Alternatives rejected.** Moving the operations to their jobs' homes (A: about 290 more lines, about 25 patch lines to re-point, and edits to the L54 guard and the golden kit). A seam that accepts a private helper (the rule is the point). Moving `_route_request` here (C3). `SERVICE` in `ops_core` (it would need a 17th shared name and a second `=` re-export exception).

**Not changed.** No rule, phrase, policy, approval or card hash; no effect path. The entry golden is untouched and replays identically.

## 2026-11-09: retakes, the base edit list keeps the last take (V3e; no measurement change, no skill changed, no lab run)

The first real render (V3b-4) kept repeated phrases: retakes were found by a rule but only proposed, and a direct `base` call cut none of
them. Decision 16 of the video plan is "keep only the last take". This entry records how.

- **D1. Where.** Detection stays a deterministic script step inside the renderer's `base`, with no model (it is computed from the words and
  their times; the transcript is read by no model in M1). It moves from `_timeline.py` to `providers/renderer/_retakes.py`, standard
  library only, with its constants and word lists in the block `retakes` of `renderer-presets.json`. `check_transcript` now returns the
  transcript's `language`. No skill, no bump, no lab run, no measurement change.
- **D2. Default cut.** Each proposal carries `default` (`cut` or `keep`) and a closed `reason`, decided by a fixed table, the first row
  that holds winning: `far`, `cut_off`, `hesitation`, `continues`, `abandoned`, `pause`, `complete`; plus `redo` (below), which turns a `far` keep into a cut. `base --retakes auto` (the default)
  cuts every `cut` proposal except those named with `--keep`, plus those named with `--confirm`; `--retakes propose` cuts only
  `--confirm` (the earlier behaviour). An id in both `--keep` and `--confirm` is refused (`retake_conflict`).
- **D3. The runtime cuts only what the person saw.** The proposal list is made with `--retakes propose`, so every word is visible in the
  review; the final `base` is called with `--retakes propose` and `--confirm` for every take cut in the review's sense: the person's
  confirmations, plus the rule's `cut` proposals the person did not keep. A take the rule finds only on the edited words, never shown, is
  never cut. A rule's take that a caption edit made stale is counted (`retakes_unmatched`) and does not stop the step; a lost take of the
  person still stops with `retake_lost`. The way back is "Keep it" on the page or `captions-edit`, recorded in a new `review.json` list
  `kept_retakes` (an old review without the key reads as empty, `REVIEW_VERSION` stays 1). The review question has one more fixed line
  with the count, and the page names who decided (the rule or the person) and why, with the words struck through when cut.
- **D4. No schema version change.** `default` and `reason` are optional keys of a proposal (always written by the new `base`; a stored
  list without them reads as `keep`), and `near` joins the kinds. `confirmed` still means "this take is cut in this list".
- **The rule** (the table of shapes is `providers/renderer/tests/test_renderer_retakes.py`, 22 rows, each run again upper-cased): a content
  token scan finds `repeat` (a run of at least `run_min` tokens that occurs again within the window, accents folded) and `restart` (a
  shorter run, or a cut-off fragment such as "pa-" then "passar", begun again and carried on); a phrase scan finds `near` (two
  consecutive phrases with the same head and at most two edits). Hesitations, fillers and cues of the language are skipped in comparisons
  and go with the cut when they lie between the takes. An immediate one-word stutter is not cut. A sentence said twice with under a
  second between is kept (emphasis or a quote), and shown in the review. A short sentence followed by its own expansion is cut as a
  restart and shown in the review. A transcript with almost no punctuation never uses `abandoned`. When a take is kept by default a later
  start inside the same repeat (the shifted copy of it) is not proposed again.
- **The `redo` rule (added after the real check, which kept two whole-sentence redos as `far`).** Real check on the recording: 404 words, 4 proposals; the two cut by default (`cut_off`, `abandoned`) were right; the two kept as `far` were a sentence of about 22 words and one of about 33 words, each said again right after itself in other words with the same opening run of 4 and 5 words. `redo`, default `cut`, takes the place of `far` for a repeat or restart candidate when all hold: both takes START with the matched run (at least `run_min`): the later one a phrase, the earlier one a phrase or, as a clause (second real-check finding: "..., but <take>" stayed `far`), the words right after a connector word of the language's closed `connector` list (pt: seven words and phrases such as e, mas, porque and que, listed in the presets file; en: and, but, so, because, that; none for another language; matched after folding, a comma before it allowed and not required), with no sentence end inside the earlier take and still one phrase (an earlier take that crosses a sentence end stays `far`); the later take starts right after the earlier one (only a pause or a filler between); the earlier take is one phrase that ends at a phrase boundary (punctuation, or a pause of at least `phrase_pause`) and has at most `redo_words_max` words and `redo_seconds_max` seconds; the later phrase has at least `redo_later_share` of the earlier phrase's tokens (a short summary of a long sentence is not a redo: row 20); the earlier phrase has at least `near_min_tokens` tokens after the run (a short sentence said twice is emphasis); and the same opening run does not start three or more consecutive phrases (a series, anaphora). It is applied only to a take that `far` would keep, so every other row keeps its reason (row 6 stays `abandoned`); a take that is not redo stays `far`. `tail_propose_max` rose from 30 to 60 content tokens so a 45-word earlier take is proposed at all; a take beyond that has no proposal and is kept. New constants: `redo_words_max` 45 (bounds 30-60), `redo_seconds_max` 16.0 (12-20), `redo_later_share` 0.4. The tests add shape A, shape B, fillers ("é…", "um"), a long pause, a three-sentence anaphora, an emphatic two-sentence repeat, and two over-long takes.
- **Real check, second round (two causes found on the recording; shapes only).** After the base cut in `--retakes auto`, one passage could not be understood.
  - **Coordination (a false retake).** A sentence of the shape "A certeza de que ele nos ama e de que ele apenas permite..." was proposed as `repeat`, default cut, reason `abandoned`, cutting "de que ele nos ama e", because the run "de que ele" repeats. It is a deliberate parallel structure: two clauses with the same opening joined by a coordinating conjunction. Rule: a token-scan candidate is a coordination, and is never proposed, when all hold: the earlier take's last token (folded) is in the language's closed `conjunction` list (pt: e, ou, nem; en: and, or, nor; `*` empty); no pause of `retake_pause` or more lies between that conjunction and the later take; and at least `coordination_between_min` (2) content tokens lie between the end of the matched run and the conjunction (so "de que ele e de que ele..." with nothing between is still the restart/repeat the rule gave before: `repeat`, cut, `abandoned`). A long pause before the later run leaves the decision to the old rule. New constant `coordination_between_min` 2; new lists `conjunction` (not a class of the comparison, only looked up like `connector`). The 22 rows and every `redo` row pass unchanged; new rows: pt "e", pt "ou", en "and", en "or" (no proposal), nothing between (old answer), long pause (old answer), an unknown language (no exemption).
  - **Phantom words (first version, withdrawn in the third round).** A run of 3 or more zero-length words that all start inside a silence was dropped. The real check on that head showed the diagnosis was wrong: see "Third round" below.
- **Real check, third round (the second round's diagnosis of phantom words was wrong; counts only).**
  - **Evidence.** On the default `--timestamps word` transcript of the real recording there were 7 zero-length runs of 3 or more words and 68 zero-length words of 426. Each run sat at the end of a silence and was the opening of a sentence whose next words follow at once; re-transcribing short clips alone returned the same words with real durations, so they were spoken, and whisper's token timestamps had collapsed them and compressed the following words by up to about 4 s. The first rule dropped all 7 runs (52 real words). With `--timestamps dtw` those runs disappear (the collapsed passage has sensible times) and one zero-length run is left, at the very end of the audio: it repeats, word for word, the 20-word sentence just before it, and lies in a silence (a known whisper repetition hallucination at the end of a file). With dtw a word ends where the next begins inside a segment, so the gaps between words of a segment are 0.
  - **The phantom rule now.** A run is phantom only when it is a repetition hallucination: `phantom_run_min` (3) or more consecutive words, each at most `phantom_max_seconds` (0.02 s) long, all starting inside a silence (with `phantom_edge_seconds`, 0.05 s, of slack), AND its folded text repeats the words said just before it: its first `phantom_repeat_min` (new, 5; allowed 4 to 8) tokens, or all of them when the run has fewer, occur in order among the last `max(2 x the run's tokens, 40)` tokens before the run. A three-word run that fully repeats the three words before it, in a silence, is dropped; a shorter run is kept; a run that repeats nothing is kept. Every other zero-length run is handled as before the second round (spread by `normalise_words`). A repeat in speech (no silence over it) is kept. Dropped words keep their indices and are listed in `normalisation.phantom_words`, shown as left out (`phantom`) in the review. `test_a_transcript_with_the_shapes_of_a_real_one_renders` has its original assertion again: its zero-length runs do not repeat the words before them.
  - **The video flow transcribes with dtw.** `runtime/step_media.py` `_transcribe` already passed `--timestamps dtw` (since V1b1-4); the real check had used a scratch script on the provider's default `word`. Nothing to wire in the runtime; the runtime test already asserts the argument. The provider's default stays `word` (plan check C3: the caller chooses), its README now records the evidence above. All four models of `models.json` have a `dtw_preset`; a model without one is refused with `usage` (a clear refusal, no fallback to `word`).
  - **Shape B on dtw timings.** The condition that failed: `redo` required the earlier take to be a single phrase. A phrase also ends at a pause of `phrase_pause` (0.6 s) or more, and with dtw a word stretched over a silence carries that pause inside the take, so a 33-word earlier take with one such pause was two phrases and fell to `far`. Fix: the earlier take may be cut into several phrases by pauses inside it; a sentence end (`final` or `cut` mark) or a hesitation or cue inside it still makes it more than one take (the "crosses a sentence end" row stays `far`). The check that a clause follows a connector word stays. Reproduced with fictional rows with zero gaps and a 0.8 s silence inside one stretched word (after a comma, and mid-clause); every existing row, with gaps and with zero gaps, is unchanged.
- **Constants** (block `retakes`; every one is the spec's starting value, none was moved, except `tail_propose_max` 60 and the three `redo_*` above): `window_seconds` 20.0, `run_min` 3,
  `restart_min` 2, `fragment_min_chars` 2, `tail_propose_max` 60 (was 30), `tail_cut_max` 6, `cut_seconds_max` 10.0, `redo_words_max` 45, `redo_seconds_max` 16.0, `redo_later_share` 0.4, `retake_pause` 1.0, `coordination_between_min` 2,
  `phrase_pause` 0.6, `near_min_tokens` 4, `near_head_tokens` 2, `near_ratio` 0.8, `near_edits_max` 2, `near_gap_max` 3.0,
  `punctuation_min_share` 0.03, `proposals_max` 100 (a test compares it with the bound of `check_edl`). Tuning is allowed only within
  `run_min` 3-4, `restart_min` 2-3, `tail_cut_max` 4-8, `cut_seconds_max` 6-15, `retake_pause` 0.9-1.5, `phrase_pause` 0.5-0.8 and
  `near_ratio` 0.75-0.9, `redo_words_max` 30-60 and `redo_seconds_max` 12-20, and only against the table of shapes, never against a real transcript.
- **Measured and not measured.** Measured: the 22 shapes of the table, on synthetic words with fixed times, offline. Not measured at
  the time of this entry: any real transcript (how whisper.cpp writes a Portuguese cut-off, whether it writes fillers and stutters, how
  many takes the rule finds on a real recording), and every constant on speech. That is the real check before merge, which reports
  counts only; no word, path or media id of a recording is in the repository.
- **Cost (principle 13).** Saved: the person no longer has to find and confirm each repeat, and a direct `base` cuts retakes. Given up: a
  deliberate repetition that the rule reads as a retake is cut until the person keeps it. Bounded by: the closed defaults, the 10 s and
  6-token limits, `propose` in the runtime (only what the person saw is cut) and the review line on every take.
- **Not done in V3e.** No way to keep a take by message on the phone (the count line is shown; a reply form comes after M1). A false
  start the transcriber left out has no words, so no text rule can see it.

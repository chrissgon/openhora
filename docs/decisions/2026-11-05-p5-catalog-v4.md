## 2026-11-05: catalog version 4 and prompt rule 3 (P5; a draft that merges only after the measurement; 0 model calls, 0 lab runs, fingerprint unchanged)

**What it is.** The fifth package of the reading fix that follows the first live results of the interpreter (`SPEC-ACEITE-FIX-READING`, item P5; `SPEC-P5-CATALOG-V4`): `operations.LINE_CATALOG` goes from 3 to 4, so the interpreter's model is served a catalog in which `items` is the mail and a new read `agenda` is the calendar, the routine rows say a routine is a routine, a series of the calendar is an event, a name that looks like a file's is a file, and the six mail act ops take the account word; and rule 3 of the prompt gains a second exception. **The edits.** (1) `items` is mail: its `about` lists the numbered messages of the mail snapshots (a line that asks for messages and events together is `items` too) and says that in `state` only the words for unread or read belong ("o último" and "the latest" are a count, not a state); the calendar alone is the new row `agenda` (class R, `since` 4, privacy `account`, cost `provider`, slots `target` (param `match`) and `day` (param `day_span`), `order` 102). (2) `routine-pause`, `routine-cancel` and `routine-change` say "only when the line names a routine (rotina, routine) or its number" and that an event of the calendar, once or repeating, is `event-cancel` or `event-change`; `event-cancel` says a whole repeating series too is its (target holds the event's words).

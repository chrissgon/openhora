## 2026-10-30: A bare word on a card that is not the last message sends the card again (ACEITE-37; limit L64)

**Decision (the maintainer, 2026-10-30, scenario 37 of the acceptance script: "fix the code").** A line that is, whole, a closed approve or reject word, with no position and no reply, on a messaging platform whose acts are not replies, while no card a word could decide is the last message, decides nothing. Code answers it, before any model: one open card that offers that act is sent again (a new ledger record, a new message id, new action ids and the hash of the decision as it is now) with one sentence, `WORD_CARD_AGAIN`; several open cards get the numbered list. The next word, or the new card's button, decides the card that was just shown. Before this, such a word went on to the interpreter and could reach a paid call.

**Where it lives.** A new pending kind of the row `frame`, `decision-card-again`, placed right after `research-run` and before `decision-card-revise` (`runtime/turn_order.py` `FRAME_KINDS`; code in `runtime/messaging_turn.py` and `Bridge.word_again` and `Bridge.question_waits` in `runtime/messaging_bridge.py`). It is a kind, and not a branch of `word_last`, because a no word while a search runs must still stop the search: `research-run` reads it first, and that order belongs in the table where a swap test pins it. A branch in `word_last` would have run before `research-run` and needed a new read-only "is a search running" call in the operations layer. It is the eighth kind and never was a row of its own, so nothing is retired.

**What is not taken.** A position (`aprova o 1`), a line that is no bare word, a reply to any message (a reply to a card still decides that card by its own hash; a reply to a message that is not a card stays as it was), a plan that still waits (the row `plan-bare-word` answers, L65), an open question's card, a phrase about the file just sent, a card that does not offer the act, a card that closed or was not sent whole, an e-mail-shaped platform, a group chat, an unlisted sender and a forwarded line.

**Principle 11.** The old card cannot be approved by a typed word: `word_cards` keeps only the newest record of each decision, so the first record is never the one a word finds, even with `last_sent` planted back to its id (tested, with a vacuity case). The old card's own button and a reply to it stay live, each bound to the hash recorded for that card; a decision that changed gets `CHANGED` and a new card as before. The card shown by the re-send carries the hash of the decision as it is now, so the content approved is the content shown.

**Golden.** Zero existing rows changed; 24 rows added in `runtime/tests/entry_ext/aceite-37.*`. The line costs 0 model calls: it is claimed in the row `frame` of the stage `bridge`, before the rows `reply` and `say` and the whole operations stage.

**Left as they are (the supervisor's answers to the open points, by their recommendations).** A card of any age is sent again (no limit: a re-send decides nothing, and an old open card is still open). A reply to a message that is not a card, with a card open, goes on as today. The sentence `WORD_CARD_AGAIN` is adopted as written.

**Docs.** Only the text of L64 changed (`contracts/runtime.md`); its generated block in `docs/architecture/platform/runtime.md` and the phrase snapshot follow from the batch keeper.

**Review round (the maintainer, 2026-10-30, "yes, both").** (1) An open question keeps the word: when the last message is a question the line may answer, a bare word brings no card back and the line goes on as it did before. Which message is a question is read from the ledger's record kinds (`attachment`, a router question's `question`, `plan-confirm`) and from the operations layer's memory of its lists (`ops.list_waits`: the list of events, "which one do you want to cancel?", and the list of files, each valid for the next line only) and drafts (a question about a reminder or a write that the line drops), never from the text of a message. A delivered reminder, a greeting or another plain sentence is no question: the card still comes back. The record kinds alone could not know a list question (it is a plain reply with no ledger record), so the operations layer got one read-only question, `list_waits`. (2) A line in question form ("sim?", "pode?", "nao?", "can you approve", "could you cancel", and in Portuguese "poderia aprovar", "consegue aprovar"; "pode aprovar" and "pode cancela" stay imperatives) is no card word, on a card that is the last message and on the re-send alike; `sim!`, `sim.`, `SIM`, `ok`, `pode` and `yes` stay forms. Two readers that keep a line away from other things (the change of a card, the claim of a research answer) keep the old reading through `card_shape`. 0 existing golden rows changed. (3) A numbered list that became one card sends that card even if it was sent again a moment ago.

**Last review round (2026-10-30/31).** (1) A question glyph anywhere in a card-shaped line makes it a question: `?`, the fullwidth and small forms, the red and white question ornaments, the double and exclamation-question marks and the interrobang; `sim!`, `sim.` and `sim` with an emoji stay forms. (2) A bare no in question form ("nao?") no longer reaches the no-word path of the row `plan-bare-word` while a plan waits: it dropped the plan and said "All right, I did nothing" with no model; now it goes on to the model like any free line, and a bare yes in question form keeps the sentence `PLAN_NOT_LAST`. `sim?` on a last "Posso?" and the stop word of a search read on `nao?` are left to the maintainer. (3) A list is the open question only while it is the last message the bridge sent (`Bridge.note_list`): a reminder delivered after it or any later message closes it, and a plain listing of the calendar is not such a list. (4) The two readers that keep `card_shape` and the count of lines since a list are pinned by tests.

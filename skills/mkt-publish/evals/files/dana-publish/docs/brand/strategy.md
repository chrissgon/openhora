- Owner: brand-strategy
- Subject: person

## Goal
People using tinykv (https://code.example/dana-example/tinykv-dana-example) [1].

## Audiences
1. Backend engineers who want fewer dependencies [1].
2. Database people who care about latency [1].

## Content pillars
Three posts per week, one per pillar, in this order: Small tools, Database performance, AI for databases built in public [1].
One language per post. Rotation: week A EN, PT, EN; week B PT, EN, PT [1].

| Pillar | Role | Proof | Examples from real material |
|--------|------|-------|-----------------------------|
| Small tools | leads to tinykv | tinykv [2] | a tinykv release; why a tool has zero dependencies |
| Database performance | reach | 64% p99 cut, self-reported [2] | what a talk or a person taught Dana, credited by name |
| AI for databases, built in public | reach | none yet; work in progress shown in public [2] | an experiment, what failed |

## What may and may not be claimed
- May: tinykv facts; self-reported numbers as Dana's experience, without the employer [2].
- May not: "expert" in AI for databases; employer names; money figures that were never measured [2].
- Links go in the first comment, never in the post body [1].

## Risks
- The AI pillar goes quiet in weeks with nothing shipped. That week's post is a behind-the-scenes of work in progress, never a claim without an artifact [1].

# Ideal customer profile: the Lousa app for the individual teacher

- Owner: biz-icp-positioning
- Language: en
- Status: hypothesis
- Date: 2028-01-04
- Offer: the Lousa app; size band: self-employed teachers, no schools; founder access: the owner's former students and a profile with 900 followers

## Primary profile
- Segment and size: self-employed English teachers in Brazil, working alone [1]
- Job to be done: prepare a lesson with slides and a PDF in minutes instead of hours [1]
- Pain and evidence: 61% spend more than 5 hours a week preparing lessons, measured by the Teachers Institute [1]
- Today they use: a rival app at R$ 39,90 per month after its introductory term [2]
- Decider: the teacher herself [1]
- Triggers: the start of a school term, when new classes begin [1]
- Where to find them: teacher associations and their chapters, events with dates [1]

## Validation plan
- Interviews: 8 with the primary profile, found through teacher associations
- Questions (past behaviour only): 1. When did you last prepare a lesson from scratch, and how long did it take? 2. What did you pay for materials last month?
- Validated if: 5 of 8 name lesson preparation as their longest weekly task
- Rejected if: fewer than 3 of 8 name it
- Results: empty

## Unknowns
- Whether teachers pay for materials from their own pocket every month.

## Assumptions
- Assumption: the teacher is the only decider.

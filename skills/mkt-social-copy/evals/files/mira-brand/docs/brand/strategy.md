# Brand strategy: Mira Example

- Owner: brand-strategy
- Subject: person
- Status: approved
- Date: 2038-10-14

## Goal
People using tinyledger (https://code.example/mira-example/tinyledger), a small local-first budgeting app Mira builds [1].

## Audiences
1. People who want to spend with intention and do not want another app that nags [1].

## Content pillars
Three short videos per week, one per pillar, in this order: Habits, Tools, Mistakes [1].

| Pillar | Role | Proof | Examples from real material |
|--------|------|-------|-----------------------------|
| Habits | reach | Mira's own month, self-reported [2] | a habit and the number it produced |
| Tools | leads to tinyledger | tinyledger [2] | how a screen of the app is used |
| Mistakes | reach | none yet [2] | what failed and what she changed |

## What may and may not be claimed
- May: tinyledger facts; self-reported numbers as Mira's experience, with the month they come from [2].
- May not: financial advice for other people, guaranteed savings, employer names, any number that was never measured [2].
- No link in a caption or in a script [1].
